      Lucid Diagnostics Provides Business Update and Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

      News provided by

      Lucid Diagnostics

      Nov 12, 2026, 08:00 ET

      Share this article

        •

      Share this article

        •

      EsoGuard® now fully reimbursed by Medicare nationwide, launching a new phase of accelerating commercialization with record monthly test volume to start the fourth quarter

      Conference call and webcast to be held today, November 12, at 8:30 AM EST

      NEW YORK, Nov. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Lucid Diagnostics Inc. (Nasdaq: LUCD) ("Lucid" or the "Company"), a commercial-stage, cancer prevention medical diagnostics company and subsidiary of PAVmed Inc. (Nasdaq: PAVM) ("PAVmed"), today provided a business update and reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

      Conference Call and Webcast

      The webcast will take place on Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 8:30 AM EST and will be accessible in the investor relations section of the Company's website at luciddx.com.  Alternatively, to access the conference call by telephone, U.S.-based callers should dial 1-800-836-8184 and international listeners should dial 1-646-357-8785. All listeners should provide the operator with the conference call name "Lucid Diagnostics Business Update" to join.

      Following the conclusion of the conference call, a replay will be available for 30 days in the investor relations section of the Company's website at luciddx.com.

      Business Highlights

      "Medicare is now paying for EsoGuard. For the first time, Medicare beneficiaries nationwide have fully reimbursed access to this groundbreaking test," said Lishan Aklog, M.D., Lucid's Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. "This breakthrough represents a seminal moment in our mission to end the scourge of esophageal cancer and marks the start of a new phase for Lucid focused on commercial acceleration and revenue growth."

      "Our pursuit of EsoGuard's enormous clinical and commercial opportunity is now substantially less constrained by external factors," Dr. Aklog added. "Lucid's near-term growth now depends almost entirely on our team's commercial and operational execution. Just a few weeks into this new chapter, our team delivered record monthly test volume in October and is approaching this opportunity with the urgency and determination it deserves. We are laser-focused on expanding access to EsoGuard for Medicare beneficiaries and translating this early momentum into revenue growth. What has been a marathon is now a sprint."

      Highlights from the third quarter and recent weeks:

      • Received first Medicare payments for the EsoGuard® Esophageal DNA Test in October. Lucid began submitting claims in late September after meeting with medical directors at Novitas, the Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) with jurisdiction over Lucid's Texas laboratory, and providing a clinical evidence dossier incorporating their feedback. Lucid has consistently received payments on these claims at EsoGuard's established Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule rate of $1,938.01 per test. This Medicare coverage through claim-by-claim adjudication is independent of the pending MolDX Local Coverage Determination (LCD) reconsideration for EsoGuard.
      • Immediately and aggressively repositioned commercial efforts to expand EsoGuard testing among at-risk Medicare beneficiaries, representing approximately half of the patients recommended for esophageal precancer testing by professional society guidelines.
      • Performed 2,871 EsoGuard tests and recognized revenue of $1.2 million in 3Q26. October was a record month for EsoGuard test volume, with more than 1,800 tests. More than 50,000 EsoGuard tests have been performed since launch.
      • Held a formal meeting with CMS leadership on the pending MolDX LCD reconsideration for EsoGuard. The discussion reinforced Lucid's confidence that the delay is attributable to an administrative backlog. Lucid expects the draft LCD to be published as that backlog begins to clear.
      • Continued to expand commercial coverage through the Concert EsoGuard coverage policy, with a total of five Concert client health plans, including the three previously reported, expected to have formally adopted it by year-end.
      • Launched Lucid's first two U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) programs, with a robust pipeline of additional VA centers behind them. Lucid continues to expect the VA to be a meaningful long-term contributor to its commercial activity and revenue.
      • Announced billing and coding milestones, including a new Category III CPT code for EsoCheck cell collection and a proposed ICD-10 diagnosis code for positive EsoGuard results, which will make EsoGuard and EsoCheck easier to bill, track and integrate into clinical practice.
      • Announced publication of an 11,991-patient real-world EsoGuard study, the largest peer-reviewed study of nonendoscopic esophageal precancer testing to date. Sampling with the EsoCheck® Esophageal Cell Collection Device was successful in 95% of patients and averaged less than two minutes. The procedure was well tolerated, with no reported device-related adverse events and consistent performance across operators, including newly trained personnel.
      • The American Gastroenterological Association (AGA) released its draft Barrett's esophagus guidelines for public comment. The draft names EsoGuard and EsoCheck as an alternative to endoscopy, with high certainty of evidence. EsoGuard is the only commercially available test cited.
      • Strengthened balance sheet with a $15.0 million private placement to existing long-term investors.

      Financial Results

      • For the three months ended September 30, 2026, EsoGuard related revenues were $1.2 million. Operating expenses were approximately $13.5 million, which included stock-based compensation expenses of $1.5 million. GAAP net loss attributable to common stockholders was approximately $6.7 million, or $(0.03) per common share.
      • As shown below and for the purpose of illustrating the effect of stock-based compensation and other non-cash income and expenses on the Company's financial results, the Company's non-GAAP adjusted loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $10.6 million or $(0.06) per common share.
      • Lucid had cash and cash equivalents of $18.0 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $34.7 million as of December 31, 2025. Subsequent to quarter end, Lucid closed a $15.0 million Series C Convertible Preferred Stock financing.
      • The unaudited financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, were filed with the SEC on Form 10-Q on November 12, 2026, and available at www.luciddx.com or www.sec.gov.

      About Lucid Diagnostics
      Lucid Diagnostics Inc. is a commercial-stage, cancer prevention medical diagnostics company, and subsidiary of PAVmed Inc. Lucid is focused on the millions of patients with GERD, also known as chronic heartburn, who are at risk of developing esophageal precancer and cancer. Lucid's EsoGuard® Esophageal DNA Test, performed on samples collected in a brief, noninvasive office procedure with its EsoCheck® Esophageal Cell Collection Device - the first and only commercially available tools designed with the goal of preventing esophageal cancer and cancer deaths through widespread, early detection of esophageal precancer in at-risk patients.

      For more information, please visit luciddx.com and for more information about its parent company PAVmed, please visit pavmed.com.
