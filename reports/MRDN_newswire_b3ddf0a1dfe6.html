Update: Meridian Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Nov. 10, 2026 8:13 AM ET Source: Meridian Holdings Inc

Third-Quarter Revenue of $46.0 Million; Net Loss Attributable to Meridian Holdings of $13.3 Million Which Reflects $11.5 Million of Non-Cash Impairment Charges, Including Brazil

Nine-Month Revenue Up 10% to $146.3 Million. Meridianbet Group Nine-Month Revenue Up 16% to $103.8 Million, Led by Online Casino Up 21%

Nine-Month Operating Cash Flow of $17.7 Million; Total Debt Down 47% to $24.1 Million; Net Debt (Non-GAAP) Down 65% to $8.1 Million, a Seventh Consecutive Quarter of Deleveraging, Without Raising Additional Equity Capital

LAS VEGAS, NV - November 10, 2026 (NEWMEDIAWIRE) - Meridian Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: MRDN) (“Meridian Holdings” or the “Company”), a sports betting and gaming group, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Zoran Milosevic, Chief Executive Officer of Meridian Holdings, commented, “This was a challenging quarter, impacted by customer-favorable sports results, regulatory developments in Brazil and higher operating costs. Brazil suspended fixed-odds betting, including online casinos, by all operators. Our core business remains healthy and outside one-off adjustments continues to generate cash to drive its own growth. Meridianbet Group grew revenue 16% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the prior year’s nine-month period, led by online casino, up 21%, period over period. At the consolidated level, Meridian Holdings generated $17.7 million in operating cash flow, sufficient to fund all of our investments while continuing to reduce debt for a seventh straight quarter. Our full-year outlook reflects the challenges faced during the third quarter. In the fourth quarter, historically our strongest, we expect higher betting volume and continued momentum in online casino. We remain focused on improving margins and driving operational efficiency through more targeted marketing and promotional costs, continued optimization of our retail footprint and expected growth in higher-margin online casino, with a goal of returning to the profitability we demonstrated in the first half of 2026.”

Third-quarter revenue was $46.0 million, down 3% year-over-year, and the net loss attributable to Meridian Holdings was $13.3 million. The Company recorded $11.5 million of non-cash impairment charges during the quarter, contributing significantly to the reported loss. Net loss was $14.7 million and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP, see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures”, below) was $0.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026. For the first nine months of 2026, revenue grew 10% year-over-year to $146.3 million, and the Company repaid $9.0 million of debt.

Of the impairment charges, $5.5 million related to the full write-off of the Brazilian gaming license and the related intangible assets after Brazil prohibited fixed-odds betting during the third quarter of 2026. A further $5.9 million related to a goodwill impairment in the reporting unit that holds RKingsCompetitions Ltd., (“RKings”), our 80% interest in Classics Holdings Co. Pty Ltd., which owns Classics For A Cause Pty Ltd (“Classics For a Cause”) and GMAG, after continued operating losses. After the $5.9 million goodwill impairment in the third quarter of 2026, goodwill on the balance sheet was $3.0 million as of September 30, 2026. Third-quarter results also reflect customer-favorable sports results, lower revenue at RKings and Classics for a Cause, and higher operating costs, including marketing around the FIFA World Cup at Meridianbet Group and bad-debt expense at GMAG.

Third Quarter and Nine-Month 2026 Financial Highlights

  • Company revenue of $46.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $1.4 million or 3% year-over-year, as revenue at RKings and Classics for a Cause fell $2.2 million, or 19%, to $9.1 million. Revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $146.3 million, up 10% from $133.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
    • Meridianbet Group grew revenue 2% to $33.1 million for the three months ended September 30, led by online casino, up 17% to $17.1 million. Compared with the third quarter of 2025, new registrations rose 25% to 458,680 and customers deposited $101.1 million during the quarter, up 9%. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Meridianbet Group revenue rose 16% to $103.8 million, and online casino revenue rose 21% to $46.9 million.
    • Average deposit levels at Meridianbet Group rose 18% in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the third quarter of 2025. Expanse Studios grew its revenue from third-party operators by 114%. In addition, MexPlay in Mexico grew revenue 55%, with first-time depositors up 35%, each in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the third quarter of 2025.
  • Net loss attributable to Meridian Holdings for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $13.3 million, or $(1.05) per basic and diluted share, including $11.5 million of non-cash impairment charges, compared with net income of $0.6 million, or $0.05 per basic and diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Net loss attributable to Meridian Holdings for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $8.8 million, or $(0.70) per basic and diluted share, compared with a net loss of $3.2 million, or $(0.29) per basic and diluted share.
  • Operating cash flow of $17.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, up 3% year-over-year from $17.1 million, fully funded the Company’s investment activities and $9.0 million in debt repayment. Free cash flow (non-GAAP, see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures”, below), defined as operating cash flow less capital expenditure of $4.6 million and lease repayments of $2.2 million, increased to $10.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, from $2.5 million in the prior-year period, when capital expenditure totaled $12.7 million.
  • Gross profit was $24.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $26.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, with gross margin of 54.0%, compared with 55.8% in the prior year’s period, reflecting the lower sports betting margin after customer-favorable sports results. Gross profit for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, rose 6% to $79.8 million. Net loss was $14.7 million and Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) was $0.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with net income of $0.4 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $4.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025 (on the current definition; see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures”, below). For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net loss was $10.6 million and Adjusted EBITDA was $12.6 million, compared with a net loss of $3.6 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $14.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The third-quarter decrease of $4.5 million in Adjusted EBITDA reflected $1.6 million of lower gross profit, mainly in online sports betting and at RKings and Classics for a Cause, partly offset by higher online casino gross profit; $0.9 million of higher bad-debt expense, primarily related to additional allowances for doubtful accounts and receivable write-offs in GMAG's B2B gaming content resale business; $0.4 million of higher marketing expenses, primarily related to the FIFA World Cup; $0.4 million of higher rent and utilities, mainly due to new betting shops; $0.3 million of higher salaries; and approximately $0.9 million of higher other operating costs.
  • The Company had cash and cash equivalents of $16.0 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $7.8 million of bank and other loans. Total debt was $24.1 million as of September 30, 2026, down 47% from September 30, 2025, and net debt (non-GAAP, see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures”, below) was $8.1 million, down 65% from September 30, 2025. The quarter ended September 30, 2026 represented the Company's seventh consecutive quarter of deleveraging. Bank and other loans fell 55% since December 31, 2025, and interest expense for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, fell 74% to $0.9 million. The Company believes its cash on hand is sufficient to meet its current working capital and capital expenditure requirements for a period of at least twelve months.
  • In September 2026, the Company extended the remaining $15.6 million of acquisition consideration owed to the former owners of Meridianbet Group, who are related parties, to November 1, 2031, with no interest. The Company may settle it earlier at its discretion.
  • Shares outstanding as of September 30, 2026, were 12,681,533, up 0.10% in the quarter and 0.32% since December 31, 2025. The Company raised no equity capital in 2026, made no share repurchases in the quarter and reports share-count figures every quarter.

Segment Performance

  • Meridianbet Group: Revenue of $33.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, up 2% year-over-year, representing 72% of Company revenue for the period. Online casino revenue rose 17% to $17.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, online casino revenue rose 21% to $46.9 million compared with the same period of 2025; the casino added new game providers in the third quarter and offered more than 2,500 games as of September 30, 2026.
    • Online sports betting revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, fell 15% year-over-year, or $1.6 million, to $8.7 million, as customer-favorable sports results lowered the online sports betting margin to 7.4%, compared with 8.2% in the third quarter of 2025. Sequentially, online sports betting revenue declined by $4.4 million, from $13.1 million in the second quarter of 2026 to $8.7 million in the third quarter of 2026. Betting volume was broadly stable, while the online sports betting margin decreased to 7.4% from 10.7% in the second quarter of 2026. Third-quarter retail revenue, from retail sports betting and retail casino, was $6.5 million, compared with $6.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
    • For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with the nine months ended September 30, 2025, online sports betting revenue rose 12% to $33.5 million, helped by wider live streaming coverage, with a margin of 8.9%, compared with 9.1% a year earlier. Over the same period, retail casino revenue rose 24% to $12.5 million, helped by more than 100 new slot machines.
    • In the third quarter of 2026, compared with the third quarter of 2025, customers deposited $101.1 million, up 9%; online casino gross gaming revenue (stakes less winnings paid, before bonuses and gaming taxes) rose 17% to $18.0 million; and the average deposit per depositor (total deposits divided by the number of depositors in the quarter) rose 18% to $457.
    • Meridianbet Group segment income from operations was $0.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $4.6 million in the prior-year period, primarily reflecting lower online sports betting margins resulting from customer-favorable sports outcomes and higher operating expenses, including increased marketing costs associated with the FIFA World Cup, higher rent and utility expenses related to the expansion of retail betting operations, and increased personnel and other operating costs.
  • Expanse Studios (included in Meridianbet Group results above): In the third quarter of 2026, compared with the third quarter of 2025, the Company's game studio grew revenue from third-party operators 114%, with unique players up 52%, pay-in (total stakes placed on Expanse games by third-party operators' players) up 104% and gross gaming revenue up 71%. The studio released 14 new games in the third quarter of 2026, including Wilder Icy Fruits and Throne of Ra, and had 101 proprietary titles as of September 30, 2026. In the third quarter of 2026, the studio received further certification in Sweden. Gates of Olympia, a new Expanse game, became one of the three most popular games at Meridianbet's online casino in the first nine months of 2026.
  • RKings and Classics for a Cause: Combined revenue of $9.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, down 19% year-over-year, primarily reflecting lower transaction volumes and average customer spending due to increased market competition, changes in customer purchasing behavior and reduced effectiveness of certain digital advertising channels, which adversely affected customer acquisition and engagement. RKings and Classics for a Cause represented approximately 20% of the Company’s revenue in the third quarter of 2026. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with the same period of 2025, segment revenue was $32.0 million, down 3%. Amendments to Australia's Interactive Gambling Act that apply to trade promotion gambling services will take effect on January 1, 2027. Classics for a Cause is working on changes to its business model to comply with the new regulatory requirements. The Company continues to assess the potential impact, as described in its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Segment income from operations was $0.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.
  • GMAG (including MexPlay): Revenue of $3.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, up 5% year-over-year, representing 8% of Company revenue for the period, with gross margin of 35.8%, compared with 33.1% for the three months ended September 30, 2025. In the third quarter of 2026, the segment deployed 2,311 new games, up 25% from the third quarter of 2025, and added 10 new content providers. MexPlay, the segment's online casino in Mexico, grew revenue 55% year-over-year to $0.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, with first-time depositors up 35% and registrations up 19% compared with the third quarter of 2025. The segment's loss from operations of $0.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, included $0.8 million of bad-debt expense.

Brazil

On September 25, 2026, Brazil's Provisional Measure No. 1,394/2026 was issued, prohibiting fixed-odds betting, including online casinos, by all operators in the country. Operators were required to take their platforms offline and return player balances in early October 2026, and the measure terminated existing federal authorizations 30 days after publication, without reimbursement of the authorization fees already paid. The Company suspended its operations in Brazil in line with the measure and wrote off the Brazilian license and related intangible assets in full, a non-cash charge of $5.5 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026. Revenue from Brazil was not material to the Company. The Company does not expect to resume its gaming operations in Brazil during the remainder of 2026. The Company intends to keep a limited local presence in Brazil while Congress and the courts decide on the measure, with an office and minimal operating costs, and will decide its next steps in Brazil based on their outcome.

Updated Full-Year 2026 Preliminary Outlook

The Company is updating the second-half 2026 outlook it issued on July 29, 2026, with a full-year outlook, below. The second-half 2026 outlook should no longer be relied upon. The Company now expects full-year 2026 revenue of between $195 million and $198 million, assuming that the Company does not resume operations in Brazil in 2026. The revision reflects the suspension of the Company's operations in Brazil, lower expected revenue at RKings and Classics for a Cause, and third-quarter sports results that favored customers. The outlook assumes continued lower revenue at RKings and Classics for a Cause in the fourth quarter. However, supported by an expected increase in betting volume during the fourth quarter, which has historically been the strongest quarter of the year for revenue, the Company anticipates a modest increase in sports betting revenue compared with the third quarter, alongside continued growth in online casino revenue.

William Scott, President, Chief Financial Officer, Treasurer and Chairman of the Board of Meridian Holdings, commented, “We ended the quarter with cash of $16.0 million, compared with $7.8 million of bank and other loans, and total debt of $24.1 million, down from $34.7 million at the start of the year. We reduced our debt using internally generated cash, without raising equity capital in 2026. In September 2026, we agreed with the former owners of Meridianbet Group to extend the repayment of the remaining $15.6 million we owe them to November 2031, with no interest. The extension allows us to keep investing where we are growing.”

About Meridian Holdings

Meridian Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: MRDN) is a sports betting and gaming group headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada. The Company operates online and retail sports betting and casino gaming in 19 jurisdictions across Europe, Africa and Central and South America. Its betting and casino brands include Meridianbet, founded in Serbia in 2001 and active in 18 jurisdictions, and MexPlay in Mexico. RKings runs pay-to-enter prize competitions in the United Kingdom, and Classics for a Cause runs trade promotions in Australia. The Company's B2B businesses include Expanse Studios, which develops and licenses casino games, and GMAG, which resells third-party gaming content, mainly in Asia-Pacific. For more information, visit www.meridian-holdings.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Net Debt, Net Debt Leverage Ratio and Free Cash Flow are non-Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP) financial measures presented as supplemental indicators of the Company's performance and capital structure.

EBITDA represents net income (loss) before interest expense, interest income, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA represents EBITDA further adjusted to exclude stock-based compensation expense, unrealized foreign exchange gains or losses, severance costs related to the termination of executive officers and directors, impairment losses on goodwill and other intangible assets, and restructuring costs, which include charges or expenses attributable to acquisition-related costs. Beginning in the first quarter of 2026, the Company changed its definition of Adjusted EBITDA to exclude unrealized foreign exchange gains and losses. Management believes this adjustment facilitates period-to-period comparisons of operating performance because these non-cash gains and losses primarily reflect fluctuations in foreign currency exchange rates. The Company recast the prior-period Adjusted EBITDA amounts presented below to conform to the current definition. Adjusted EBITDA for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025 was $4.8 million and $14.0 million, respectively, after excluding unrealized foreign exchange gains of $0.9 million and $0.8 million, respectively. The Company previously reported Adjusted EBITDA of $5.7 million and $14.8 million for those periods, respectively.

Net Debt is defined as total debt, less cash and cash equivalents. Net Debt Leverage Ratio is defined as Net Debt at the balance sheet date divided by annualized Adjusted EBITDA for the period then ended, calculated for the third quarter of 2026 as Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, multiplied by four-thirds. Free Cash Flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less cash paid for intangible assets, cash paid for property, plant and equipment, and repayment of leases. Free Cash Flow does not represent cash available for discretionary spending, because it does not deduct required debt repayments.

We present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because we believe these measures provide investors with additional insight into our core operating performance by excluding the effects of financing costs, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, and, in the case of Adjusted EBITDA, certain non-cash and other specified items. We present Net Debt and the Net Debt Leverage Ratio to provide investors with a better understanding of our indebtedness, net of cash and cash equivalents, and our leverage relative to earnings. We present Free Cash Flow to provide insight into the cash generated from operating activities after investments in intangible assets and property, plant and equipment, and lease payments. We believe these non-GAAP financial measures, when considered together with our GAAP financial results, provide useful supplemental information for evaluating our operating performance, cash generation, liquidity and financial position.

These measures are non-GAAP measures, are unaudited, and have inherent limitations as analytical tools. These measures should not be considered in isolation, or as substitutes for analysis of the Company's results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations include: they do not reflect cash expenditures or future capital requirements; they do not reflect working capital requirements or contractual commitments; they do not reflect significant interest expense, principal, or income tax payments; although depreciation and amortization are non-cash charges, the assets being depreciated or amortized may require replacement in the future; and other companies may calculate these measures differently, limiting comparability. The Company's presentation of these measures should not be construed as implying that future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items.

For additional information regarding these non-GAAP financial measures, please refer to the sections titled “Reconciliation of U.S. GAAP Net Income (Loss) to EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA (Unaudited)” and “Reconciliation of Net Debt and Leverage Calculation (Unaudited)”, included at the end of this release. Free Cash Flow is reconciled to net cash provided by operating activities in the table “Reconciliation of Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities to Free Cash Flow (Unaudited)”, also at the end of this release.

The Company does not provide a reconciliation of forward-looking Adjusted EBITDA to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure because it is unable to do so without unreasonable effort due to the inherent uncertainty in forecasting certain items, including, but not limited to, stock-based compensation, transaction-related expenses, and other non-recurring or non-cash items; accordingly, a reconciliation is not available without unreasonable effort, and the unavailable information could have a material impact on the Company's future GAAP financial results.
