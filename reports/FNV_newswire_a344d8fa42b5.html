      News provided by

      Franco-Nevada Corporation

      Nov 11, 2026, 17:30 ET

      Share this article

      (in U.S. dollars unless otherwise noted)

      Tracking towards the mid-range of annual guidance

      TORONTO, Nov. 11, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Franco-Nevada generated strong Q3 financial results. Revenue, cash flow and earnings were all higher than the prior-year period, driven by stronger gold and oil prices. "Precious metal GEO deliveries were slightly below expectations in the period, although strong oil prices boosted our diversified revenue, and we are now tracking towards the mid-range of our annual guidance," stated Paul Brink, President & CEO. "During the quarter, First Quantum continued processing stockpiles at Cobre Panamá and the Panamanian Interministerial Commission recommended that the Government begin formal negotiations with First Quantum regarding the future of the mine. We saw several project advancements in our portfolio, including at Antapaccay, Copper World, and Ring of Fire, and we took another step in growing our Australian business with a second tranche of financing for the development of the Bullabulling deposit. With $4.8 billion in available capital and no debt, the Company has ample firepower to take advantage of a strong pipeline of deal opportunities."

      Financial Highlights –Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025

      • $549.3 million in revenue, +13%.
      • 130,435 GEOs1 sold, -6%.
      • 120,857 Net GEOs1 sold, -3%.
      • $429.2 million in operating cash flow, +23%.  
      • $493.9 million ($2.56/share) in Adjusted EBITDA2 , +15%.
      • $395.5 million ($2.05/share) in net income, +38%.
      • $364.5 million ($1.89/share) in Adjusted Net Income2, +33%.
      • $4.8 billion in Available Capital3 as at September 30, 2026.

      Financial Highlights –YTD 2026 compared to YTD 2025

      • $1,780.9 million in revenue, +45% – a new YTD record.
      • 399,193 GEOs sold, +6%.
      • 369,082 Net GEOs sold, +9%.
      • $1,432.1 million in operating cash flow, +34% – a new YTD record.
      • $1,615.5 million in Adjusted EBITDA or $8.38/share, +45% – a new YTD record.
      • $1,218.1 million in net income or $6.32/share, +64% – a new YTD record.
      • $1,172.0 million in Adjusted Net Income or $6.08/share, +63% – a new YTD record.

      In Q3 2026, we recognized revenue of $549.3 million, an increase of 13% from Q3 2025, and sold 130,435 GEOs, a decrease of 6% from Q3 2025. We benefited from higher commodity prices and increased contributions from our PGM and energy assets compared to Q3 2025. The increase in revenue was partially offset by lower contributions from Guadalupe-Palmarejo, Antapaccay, and Cobre Panamá. The decrease in GEOs sold in Q3 2026 was driven by lower GEO contributions from our gold stream segment and iron ore assets, which was partially offset by higher contributions from our energy assets as well as the more favorable silver to gold conversion ratio based on our assumed commodity prices for 2026 when converting our silver revenue to GEOs.

      Precious Metal assets accounted for 84% of our revenue in Q3 2026 (70% gold, 12% silver, and 2% PGM). Revenue was sourced 89% from the Americas (39% South America, 23% Canada, 17% U.S. and 10% Central America & Mexico).

      Portfolio Additions

      • Acquisition of Additional Royalty from Discovery Mining Ltd. on the Porcupine Complex – Ontario, Canada: Subsequent to quarter-end, on October 22, 2026, we amended our royalty agreement with Discovery Mining Ltd. ("Discovery") to extend our existing royalty interest on the Porcupine Complex to cover certain mineral properties purchased by Discovery adjacent to the Dome Mine in Timmins, Ontario, in exchange for $8.0 million payable to Discovery.

      • Financing Package with Minerals 260 Limited on the Bullabulling Gold Project – Australia: On September 13, 2026, we acquired, through a wholly owned Australian subsidiary, a $121.0 million (A$170.0 million) 1.45% gross royalty over Minerals 260 Limited's ("Minerals 260") Bullabulling gold project, incremental to the already existing 2.45% gross royalty acquired on February 26, 2026. The additional royalty increased our gross royalty interest over the Bullabulling project to 3.90% and covers a Bullabulling land package inclusive of all mineral resources, plus an area of interest that includes the Phoenix, Bacchus, Dicksons, Kraken and Gibraltar deposits. Upon production of 6.0 Moz of gold from royalty lands, the Bullabulling gross royalty will step down from 3.90% to 2.75%. Additionally, the Company subscribed for an additional $21.0 million (A$30.0 million) of Minerals 260's ordinary shares which closed subsequent to quarter-end, on November 6, 2026.

      Cobre Panamá Update

      Cobre Panamá remains in a phase of Preservation and Safe Management ("P&SM") with production halted.

      On September 30, 2026, First Quantum reported that the Interministerial Commission formally delivered its final report on Cobre Panamá to the President, marking the Government of Panama's ("GOP") transition from technical assessment to the decision-making phase. The Interministerial Commission recommended assessing the feasibility of entering into a new agreement with Cobre Panamá / First Quantum that would provide for the settlement of the outstanding arbitration proceedings and establish conditions for a potential restart designed to achieve an orderly, self-financed closure, while complying with the ruling of the Supreme Court of Justice and prioritizing Panama's environmental, social, and economic interests. The President is reviewing the report with the Cabinet and legal advisers.

      Panama experienced an earthquake on October 9, 2026. Cobre Panamá's facilities and infrastructure were inspected and reported to be stable and secure. Stockpile processing was temporarily suspended to allow for precautionary inspections and resumed on October 10, 2026.

      First Quantum estimates that Cobre Panamá will maintain guidance to produce between 30,000 and 40,000 tonnes of copper in 2026 from the processing of stockpiles, with the remaining stockpile balance to be processed in 2027 for a total of approximately 70,000 tonnes. Based on these estimates, Cobre Panamá stream deliveries to Franco-Nevada are expected to total approximately 23,100 gold ounces and 265,000 silver ounces. Deliveries of stream ounces to Franco-Nevada, which are determined based on the sale of copper concentrate by First Quantum under its offtake agreements, commenced in Q3 2026, with one-third of deliveries anticipated in H2 2026. During the quarter, we recorded a partial impairment reversal of $10.3 million in relation to the gold and silver ounces we expect as a result of the GOP authorization. We continue to assess our Cobre Panamá stream for further indicators of impairment reversals.

      Sustainability Updates

      We continue to be top-rated by Sustainalytics, AAA by MSCI and Prime by ISS ESG. During the quarter, we expanded the Franco-Nevada Mining Industry Scholarship Program by awarding eight new scholarships, of which four were awarded to university students and four to students enrolled in mining-related trade programs. The program now supports 23 students, seven of whom are expected to graduate from their respective programs next year. We also entered a further two-year commitment to the Enseña Perú education initiative, which supports students and teachers across the Áncash region of Peru. Subsequent to quarter-end, we entered a multi-year commitment with Minera Panamá, S.A. ("MPSA") to support community development and environmental initiatives led by MPSA, including education, workforce development, health infrastructure, reforestation and marine conservation programs in Panama.

      Q3 2026 Portfolio Updates

      Precious Metal assets: GEOs sold from our Precious Metal assets amounted to 110,904 GEOs for Q3 2026, a decrease of 7% from 119,109 GEOs in Q3 2025. This was primarily due to lower deliveries from Cobre Panamá, Guadalupe-Palmarejo, Antapaccay, Subika and Candelaria, partially offset by higher deliveries from South Arturo and Hemlo, as well as incremental contributions from Casa Berardi and Valentine, which were acquired or commenced production over the past year.

      South America:

      • Candelaria (gold and silver stream) – GEOs sold in Q3 2026 were lower than those sold in Q3 2025. Production at the mine was lower compared to prior year period, primarily due to the impact to mining operations due to heavy rainfall from severe winter storms in Chile's Atacama region resulting in lower grade feed from stockpiles.
      • Antapaccay (gold and silver stream) – GEOs sold in Q3 2026 were lower than those sold in Q3 2025, primarily due to timing of deliveries, as well as expected lower head grades, recoveries and milling throughput at the plant. Post quarter-end, Glencore announced the completion of land access agreements for the Coroccohuayco project, and that the team is working to secure final permits to enable first production, expected in H2 2029.  Coroccohuayco is expected to extend Antapaccay's life to over 40 years.
      • Antamina (22.5% silver stream) – Silver ounces sold in Q3 2026 were lower than those sold in Q3 2025. The decrease in deliveries is attributable to lower concentrator throughput at the mine. However, overall GEOs were higher due to GEO conversion differences. Post quarter-end, Antamina reported a fatal accident at its operations, with an investigation underway.
      • Tocantinzinho (gold stream) – GEOs sold in Q3 2026 were lower than those sold in Q3 2025. G Mining indicates mining activities remain closely aligned with the mine plan, enabling access to higher-grade Phase 2 mineralization and expects a significant increase in grade and production in Q4 2026.
      • Condestable (gold and silver stream) – GEOs sold in Q3 2026 were higher than those sold in Q3 2025. The stream transitioned from fixed deliveries to variable deliveries in Q2 2026. Rio2 Limited announced in August 2026 the approval for the modification of the environmental impact study for the expansion of the Condestable Mine, which will permit an increase of throughput capacity to 10,000 tpd from the current 8,400 tpd. 
      • Yanacocha (1.8% royalty) – GEOs from our Yanacocha royalty in Q3 2026 were lower than those in Q3 2025 due to lower production, as a result of mine plan sequencing. Following the success of the oxide leach, Newmont now expects production to continue in 2027, with possible further extension of mining operations beyond that.
      • PSJ Cobre Mendocino (7.5% royalty) – PSJ Cobre Mendocino (formerly San Jorge), a copper-gold project located in the province of Mendoza, Argentina, obtained approval under Argentina's Large Investment Incentive Regime (RIGI) in May 2026. A feasibility study is expected in late 2026 and initial production is planned for 2029.
      • Calcatreu (2.5% royalty) – First gold produced in June 2026. In September 2026, Patagonia Gold released an updated PEA outlining a 16-year mine life with additional exploration upside across the broader district.
      • Gurupi (0-1% royalty) – Drilling expanded mineralization at Blanket and Mandiocal and confirmed a new discovery at Grodiocal, supporting future resource growth. A PEA is expected by year-end 2026.
      • Salares Norte (1% royalties) – Gold Fields reported strong Q3 2026 results for Salares Norte and expects 550,000 to 600,000 ounces of gold-equivalent production for 2026.

      Central America & Mexico:

      • Cobre Panamá (gold and silver stream) – During the quarter, we received and sold 3,716 GEOs following the Government of Panama's approval of processing stockpiled ore. Comparatively, in Q3 2025, GEOs delivered and sold were related to the sale of copper concentrate stored at site when production was suspended in November 2023. First Quantum noted that all three processing trains had been brought on-line sequentially and that site employment had reached 3,200 people at the end of September.
      • Guadalupe-Palmarejo (50% gold stream) – GEOs sold in Q3 2026 were lower than Q3 2025. Coeur Mining reported that disruption in delivery of critical construction materials, as a result of heavy rain fall, affected planned mining in higher-grade areas.

      Canada:

      • Côté Gold (7.5% GMR) – Production (on a 100% basis) from Côté Gold in Q3 2026 was 92,000 gold ounces, lower than those in Q3 2025, where production was 106,000 gold ounces. The decrease was primarily due to a lower mill throughput during the quarter, reflecting an extended annual shutdown in August and the transition to owner-only crushing. IAMGOLD expects production to increase in Q4 2026, driven by higher grades from mine sequencing and an increased processing rate with total production for the year expected to be at the lower end of the guidance range. An updated mine plan including the Gosselin Deposit and an updated mineral reserve estimate are expected for Q4 2026. Subsequent to quarter-end, on November 5, 2026, we received notice from IAMGOLD of the exercise of its right to buy back 50% of our Côté Gold GMR.       1. Gold Equivalent Ounces ("GEOs") and Net Gold Equivalent Ounces ("Net GEOs"):

        • GEOs include Franco-Nevada's attributable share of production from our Mining and Energy assets after applicable recovery and payability factors. GEOs are estimated on a gross basis for NSRs and, in the case of stream ounces, before the payment of the per ounce contractual price paid by the Company. For NPI royalties, GEOs are calculated taking into account the NPI economics. Where the Company receives gold and silver bullion in-kind as payment for its royalties, GEOs are recognized at the time of receipt of such bullion. Silver, platinum, palladium, iron ore, oil, gas and other commodities are converted to GEOs by dividing associated revenue, which includes settlement adjustments, by the relevant gold price. Beginning in 2026, the Company adopted fixed GEO conversion ratios based on the pricing assumptions outlined in our guidance. This methodology replaces our previous methodology which was based on variable GEO conversion ratios using prevailing market prices. Our 2026 guidance, as disclosed in our 2025 MD&A filed on March 10, 2026, assumed the following commodity prices: $4,500/oz Au, $75.00/oz Ag, $2,000/oz Pt, $1,650/oz Pd, $100/tonne Fe 62% CFR China, $70/bbl WTI oil and $3.00/mcf Henry Hub natural gas. GEOs for the 2026 period are calculated based on fixed conversion ratios based on the prices assumed in this 2026 guidance.
        • Net GEOs are GEOs sold, net of direct operating costs, including for our stream GEOs, the associated ongoing cost per ounce.

      • Change in Composition of Adjusted Net Income – Gains on buy-backs of royalty and stream interests: Effective Q1 2026, the Company updated the composition of its Adjusted Net Income (and related per share and margin amounts) to no longer adjust for gains on contractual buy-backs of royalty and stream interests. Unlike less common discretionary sales of mineral interests, these transactions are evaluated by management when assessing overall returns from our royalty and stream interests, and accordingly, we believe such gains should not be eliminated for purposes of calculating Adjusted Net Income and related per share amounts, when evaluating performance for investors.
