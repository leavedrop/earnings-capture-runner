Unaudited Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026

Total revenues were RMB24.28 billion (US$3.62 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 19.1% from RMB20.38 billion for the same period of 2025 and an increase of 23.0% from RMB19.74 billion for the second quarter of 2026.

Revenues from vehicle sales were RMB21.63 billion (US$3.23 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 19.8% from RMB18.05 billion for the same period of 2025, and an increase of 26.9% from RMB17.05 billion for the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were mainly attributable to higher vehicle deliveries and higher average selling price as a result of the improvement in product mix of models.

Revenues from services and others were RMB2.65 billion (US$0.39 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 13.7% from RMB2.33 billion for the same period of 2025 and a decrease of 1.9% from RMB2.70 billion for the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year increase was primarily attributable to increased revenues from carbon credit trading.

 

3

 

Cost of sales was RMB19.68 billion (US$2.93 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 20.9% from RMB16.28 billion for the same period of 2025 and an increase of 25.6% from RMB15.66 billion for the second quarter of 2026. The quarter-over-quarter increase was mainly in line with vehicle deliveries as described above.

Gross margin was 18.9% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 20.1% for the same period of 2025 and 20.7% for the second quarter of 2026.

Vehicle margin was 13.1% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 13.1% for the same period of 2025 and 12.1% for the second quarter of 2026. The quarter-over-quarter increase was due to the improvement in product mix of models.

Services and others margin was 67.1% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 74.6% for the same period of 2025 and 75.1% for the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter decreases were primarily due to the reduction in technical research and development services revenues in the current quarter, reflecting significant milestone catch-ups in the prior-year period and prior quarter, respectively.

Research and development expenses were RMB2.85 billion (US$0.43 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 17.5% from RMB2.43 billion for the same period of 2025 and a decrease of 2.1% from RMB2.91 billion for the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year increase was mainly due to higher expenses related to the development of new vehicle models and AI-related technologies as the Company expanded its product portfolio to support future growth.

Selling, general and administrative expenses were RMB2.78 billion (US$0.41 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing an increase of 11.4% from RMB2.49 billion for the same period of 2025 and an increase of 11.2% from RMB2.50 billion for the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year increase was primarily due to higher marketing and advertising expenses and higher personnel costs related to sales functions. The quarter-over-quarter increase was primarily due to higher marketing and advertising expenses and higher commission to the franchised stores.

Other income, net was RMB0.11 billion (US$0.02 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, representing a decrease of 18.3% from RMB0.14 billion for the same period of 2025 and a decrease of 16.5% from RMB0.14 billion for the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter decreases were primarily due to the decrease in receipt of government subsidies.

Fair value (loss) gain on derivative liability relating to the contingent consideration was a gain of RMB0.16 billion (US$0.02 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with a loss of RMB0.07 billion for the same period of 2025 and a gain of RMB0.05 billion for the second quarter of 2026. This non-cash gain resulted from the fair value change of the contingent consideration related to the acquisition of DiDi Global Inc. (“DiDi”)’s smart auto business.

Loss from operations was RMB0.75 billion (US$0.11 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB0.75 billion for the same period of 2025 and RMB1.14 billion for the second quarter of 2026.

Non-GAAP loss from operations, which excludes share-based compensation expenses and fair value gain on derivative liability relating to the contingent consideration, was RMB0.77 billion (US$0.12 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with a loss of RMB0.52 billion for the same period of 2025 and a loss of RMB1.04 billion for the second quarter of 2026.

Non-GAAP net loss, which excludes share-based compensation expenses and fair value gain on derivative liability relating to the contingent consideration, was RMB0.89 billion (US$0.13 billion) for the third quarter of 2026, compared with a loss of RMB0.15 billion for the same period of 2025 and a loss of RMB1.24 billion for the second quarter of 2026.

About XPENG

XPENG is a leading global Physical AI company, dedicated to bringing artificial intelligence into the physical world to reshape future mobility and smart living. Through in-house R&D, XPENG has developed a full-stack Physical AI architecture spanning Turing AI chips, world foundation models, and highly integrated software and hardware applications. This unified technology foundation of XPENG powers an expansive product portfolio of smart EVs, robotaxis, and humanoid robots, advancing the deployment of Physical AI at scale. Headquartered in Guangzhou, China, XPENG is dual-primary listed on the New York Stock Exchange and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.
