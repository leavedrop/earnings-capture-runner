2026 – H World Group Limited (NASDAQ: HTHT and HKEX: 1179) (“ H World ”, the “ Company ”, “ Group ”,
“ we ” or “ our ”), a key player in the global hotel industry, today announced its unaudited financial
results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, H World’s
worldwide hotel network in operation totaled 14,026 hotels and 1,381,324 rooms, including 13,906 hotels from HWC and 120 hotels from
HWI. During the third quarter of 2026, our HWC business opened 664 hotels, including 4 leased and owned (“ L&O ”)
hotels, and 660 M&F hotels, and closed a total of 175 hotels, including 12 L&O hotels, and 163 M&F hotels. As of September 30,
2026, H World had a total of 2,806 unopened hotels in our pipeline, including 2,772 hotels from the HWC business and 34 hotels from the
HWI business. HWC – Third Quarter of 2026 Operational Highlights As of September 30, 2026, HWC had 13,906
hotels in operation, including 476 L&O hotels, and 13,430 M&F hotels. In addition, as of the same date, HWC had 1,356,403 hotel
rooms in operation, including 73,494 rooms under the L&O model, and 1,282,909 rooms under the M&F models. HWC had 2,772 unopened
hotels in its pipeline, including 10 L&O hotels, and 2,762 M&F hotels. The following discusses HWC’s average daily room
rate (“ ADR ”), occupancy rate and revenue per available room (“ RevPAR ”) for HWC L&O hotels,
as well as for HWC M&F hotels for the periods indicated. · The HWC ADR was RMB309 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB304 in the third quarter of 2025 and RMB298 in the previous quarter. · The occupancy rate for all HWC hotels in operation was 82.6% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 84.1% in the third quarter of
2025 and 79.8% in the previous quarter. · Blended HWC RevPAR was RMB256 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with RMB255 in the third quarter of 2025 and RMB238 in the previous
quarter. Page 2 of 21 · For all HWC hotels which had been in operation for at least 18 months, the same-hotel RevPAR was RMB248 in the third quarter of 2026,
representing a 4.4% decrease from RMB259 in the third quarter of 2025, with a 1.3% decrease in same-hotel ADR and a 2.7 percentage-point
decrease in same-hotel occupancy rate. HWI – Third Quarter of 2026 Operational Highlights As of September 30, 2026, HWI had 120 hotels
in operation, including 63 leased hotels, and 57 M&F hotels. In addition, as of the same date, HWI had 24,921 hotel rooms in operation,
including 13,441 rooms under the lease model, and 11,480 rooms under the M&F models. HWI also had 34 unopened hotels in the pipeline,
including 8 leased hotels and 26 M&F hotels. The following discusses HWI’s ADR, occupancy rate and RevPAR for leased as well
as for HWI M&F hotels (excluding hotels temporarily closed) for the periods indicated. · The HWI ADR 5 was US$132 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$131 in the third quarter of 2025 and US$137 in the
previous quarter. · The occupancy rate for all HWI hotels in operation was 74.2% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 74.6% in the third quarter of
2025 and 70.5% in the previous quarter. · Blended HWI RevPAR 5 was US$98 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$98 in the third quarter of 2025 and US$97 in
the previous quarter. Jin Hui, CEO of H World commented: “During
the third quarter of 2026, despite adverse weather conditions during the summer holiday and other unfavorable factors, our blended HWC
segment’s RevPAR delivered a stable performance year-over-year. Our blended HWC segment’s ADR rose 1.8% year-over-year, supporting

a key player in the global hotel industry. As of September 30, 2026, H World operated 14,026 hotels with 1,381,324 rooms in operation
in 21 countries. H World’s brands include HanTing Hotel, JI Hotel, Orange Hotel, Crystal Orange Hotel, IntercityHotel, Grand
JI Hotel, Hi Inn, Ni Hao Hotel, Elan Hotel, Zleep Hotels, Starway Hotel, CitiGO, Manxin Hotel, Madison Hotel, MAXX Hotel, Blossom House,
Joya Hotel, Steigenberger Hotels & Resorts, Jaz in the City, Steigenberger Icons and Song Hotels. In addition, H World also
has the rights as master franchisee for Mercure, Ibis and Ibis Styles, and co-development rights for Grand Mercure and Novotel,
in the pan-China region. H World’s business includes L&O and
M&F models. Under the L&O model, H World directly operates hotels typically located on leased or owned properties. Under the
manachise model, H World manages manachised hotels through the on-site hotel managers that H World appoints, and H World collects fees
from franchisees. Under the franchise model, H World provides training, reservations and support services to the franchised hotels, and
collects fees from franchisees but does not appoint on-site hotel managers. H World applies a consistent standard and platform across
all of its hotels. As of September 30, 2026, H World operated 6 percent of its hotel rooms under the L&O model, and 94 percent
under the M&F model. Page 9 of 21 For more information, please visit H World’s
