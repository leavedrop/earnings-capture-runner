Infleqtion Reports Third Quarter 2026 Record Revenue Results, Achieves 30-Logical-Qubit Milestone and Maintains Full-Year Revenue Guidance


Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary  

•Revenue: $9.1 million, up 25% year over year, nine-month revenue $32.6 million, up 57% year over year, 100% organic and entirely quantum. 

•Operating Loss: GAAP operating loss was $34.5 million, compared with $6.7 million in Q3 2025. Non-GAAP operating loss was $22.7 million, compared with $5.9 million in Q3 2025.  

•Cash Flow: Net cash used in operations of $16.3 million and capital expenditures of $5.2 million. 

•Balance Sheet: At quarter end, cash, cash equivalents, and available-for-sale securities totaled $560 million, with no debt. This balance included a $28 million temporary working-capital benefit from payroll taxes collected from stock-option exercises.


LOUISVILLE, Colo.—(BUSINESS WIRE)--November 16, 2026— Infleqtion, Inc. (NYSE: INFQ) ("Infleqtion" or the "Company"), a global leader in quantum computing and quantum sensing powered by neutral atom technology, today reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $9.1 million, up 25% year over year, and maintained its 2026 revenue outlook of at least $45.1 million. 


“We are building Infleqtion to lead in utility-scale quantum computing, and this quarter’s progress strengthens our confidence in that ambition,” said Matt Kinsella, CEO of Infleqtion. “Delivering 30 logical qubits is an important step on our technology roadmap. Alongside that progress, growth in our backlog and remaining performance obligations gives us confidence in a strong 2027 across computing and sensing, with computing becoming a meaningfully larger part of our business.” 


Third Quarter and Recent Business Highlights  

•Met 2026 Target of 30 Logical Qubits: Infleqtion demonstrated 30 entangled logical qubits working together in a single computation on Sqale using 80 physical qubits. An AI-assisted discovery cut the physical gates needed for a key logical operation in half, reducing opportunities for errors as the Company works toward longer computations and 100 logical qubits in 2028. 

•Quantum Computers Operational in Japan and the U.K.: Infleqtion’s QPU powers Japan’s first operational full-stack neutral atom quantum computer under the country's Moonshot program, joining Infleqtion’s deployed system at the U.K.'s National Quantum Computing Centre.  

•Expanded Computing Collaborations and Customer Application Work: Infleqtion announced collaborations with NVIDIA, Cisco and Riverlane to advance quantum networking, error correction and software integration, capabilities needed to scale quantum computers and support more demanding customer applications.  Infleqtion also launched its Quantum Application Forum series in London, bringing together companies from financial services, energy, pharmaceuticals and other industries to explore quantum computing applications. 

•$20 Million NASA Expansion Contract Funds Next Stage of Quantum Gravity Development: Infleqtion is developing quantum gravity sensing hardware with the Jet Propulsion Laboratory for NASA’s


Quantum Gravity Gradiometer Pathfinder program, bringing the cumulative value of Infleqtion’s contract to $40 million. 

•$12 Million Award Funds Deployable Quantum Spectrum System: The award funds engineering and validation of Infleqtion’s Quantum Spectrum system for a direction-finding application, which detects radio signals and determines their direction. Initial development targets defense platforms where conventional antenna size limits deployment, bringing direction-finding capabilities to smaller systems.  

•Tiqker Demonstrates Rugged Operation in U.S. Navy Exercises: Tiqker demonstrated sustained operation aboard RV Merlin under demanding maritime conditions. The demonstration supports future naval integration of GPS-independent precision timing for navigation, communications and other mission-critical systems.

“Non-GAAP Cost of revenue” is defined as cost of revenue expense adjusted to exclude, when applicable, stock-based compensation.



“Non-GAAP R&D” is defined as research and development expense adjusted to exclude, when applicable, stock-based compensation.

“Non-GAAP SG&A” is defined as selling, general and administrative expense adjusted to exclude, when applicable, stock-based compensation, acquisition and integration costs, go-public transaction expenses and former executive release payment.

“Non-GAAP Loss from operations” is defined as loss from operations adjusted to exclude, when applicable, stock-based compensation, go-public transaction expenses, acquisition and integration costs, former executive release payment and impairment of assets and goodwill.

About Infleqtion
Infleqtion, Inc. (NYSE: INFQ) is a global leader in quantum technology, delivering neutral atom solutions for quantum computing, networking, sensing, and security. With a product portfolio spanning quantum computers, quantum optical clocks, RF receivers, and inertial sensors, Infleqtion’s full-stack approach combines high-performance hardware with the company’s proprietary Superstaq quantum computing software platform. Infleqtion’s systems are already in use by the U.S. Department of War, NASA, the U.K. government, and in multiple collaborations with NVIDIA. Infleqtion, in collaboration with NVIDIA, published the world’s first demonstration of a materials science application using logical qubits. With operations in the U.S., Europe, and Asia, Infleqtion meets the demands of government and commercial customers across the space, defense, energy, finance and telecommunications sectors. For more information, visit Infleqtion.com or follow Infleqtion on LinkedIn, YouTube, and X.
