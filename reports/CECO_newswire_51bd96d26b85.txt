CECO Environmental Corp. Record Third Quarter Results Highlighted by Orders, Backlog and Adjusted EBITDA, Each Up Over 200 Percent Company Updates 2026 Outlook, Increasing Full Year Orders and Free Cash Flow Forecast Company Introduces Full Year 2027 Outlook ADDISON, Texas, Nov.  09, 2026  (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- CECO Environmental Corp. (TXSE: CECO) ("CECO," or the “Company”) , a leading environmentally focused, diversified industrial company whose solutions protect people, the environment, and industrial equipment, today reported its financial results for the third quarter of 2026. Highlights for the Quarter (1) Second quarter as a combined company following the June 1, 2026 acquisition of Thermon Orders of $777.6 million, up 234 percent; backlog of $2,193.5 million, up 205 percent Revenue of $404.4 million, up 105 percent Gross profit of $130.1 million, up 101 percent; gross margin of 32.2 percent; Non-GAAP gross profit of $146.8 million, up 127 percent; non-GAAP gross margin of 36.3 percent Net loss of $(2.0) million, compared with net income of $1.5 million; non-GAAP net income of $34.3 million, up 269 percent GAAP EPS (diluted) of $(0.03); non-GAAP EPS (diluted) of $0.60 Adjusted EBITDA of $70.1 million, up 202 percent; Adjusted EBITDA margin of 17.3% Free cash flow of $148.4 million, a $137.3 million improvement; adjusted free cash flow of $154.4 million, a $135.4 million improvement, adjusted for cash payments relating to the Thermon transaction made during the quarter Leverage of 2.0X, a reduction of 0.7X from the closing date of the Thermon acquisition (1) All comparisons are versus the comparable prior year period, unless otherwise stated. Reconciliations of GAAP (reported) to non-GAAP measures are in the attached financial tables. Todd Gleason, CECO's Chairman and Chief Executive Officer commented: “Our strong third quarter results reflect CECO’s leadership in attractive and growing niche markets across a range of resilient industrial end markets. Customers around the world rely on our solutions and expertise to meet or exceed critical environmental and process safety requirements. Our sustainable growth is a testament to the strength of our incredible portfolio, the dedication of our employees, and the support of our global partners. We have a proven track record of creating value for our customers, partners, communities and our shareholders. While recent headlines and U.S. election developments have created volatility in equity markets, which we certainly monitor, we remain focused on the factors within our control. The continued visibility we have in our $2.2 billion backlog – the vast majority of which is permitted and underway, our rapidly growing $9 billion sales pipeline, and our productivity and synergy initiatives reinforce my confidence in our strategy and my conviction in our ability to deliver significant long-term value creation.” Third quarter operating income was $10.5 million, compared with operating income of $9.4 million in the prior-year quarter. On an adjusted basis, non-GAAP operating income was $55.7 million, up $38.2 million or 218 percent compared with $17.5 million in the prior-year quarter. Net loss was $(2.0) million in the quarter, compared with net income of $1.5 million in the prior-year quarter. Non-GAAP net income was $34.3 million, an increase of $25.0 million, or 269 percent, from $9.3 million in the prior-year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA was $70.1 million, reflecting a margin of 17.3 percent, an increase of $46.9 million, or 202 percent, compared with $23.2 million in the prior-year quarter. Adjusted free cash flow in the quarter was $154.4 million, an improvement of $135.4 million compared with $19.0 million in the prior-year quarter. “Since closing the Thermon acquisition in June, we have substantially completed key integration initiatives. We remain ahead of schedule on both our cost and revenue synergies, and the combination is an excellent cultural fit. Across our entire CECO portfolio, we continue to see strong momentum driven by growth across our diverse end markets, increasing customer demand, and the continued execution of our operational initiatives, which are contributing to margin expansion and enhanced cash flow generation. Our proven operating model has delivered regular double-digit growth and meaningful margin expansion since 2021, and I expect will continue to drive strong performance in 2027 and 2028. While we are pleased to have delivered more than 100 percent revenue growth and over 200 percent growth in orders, backlog and Adjusted EBITDA during the third quarter, we are even more excited about the future. The strategic investments we have made to strengthen and expand our industrial leadership into new markets position us to drive even higher levels of performance in the years to come,” added Gleason. 2026 Full Year Guidance Update The Company is updating its full-year 2026 outlook as a result of the strong year-to-date performance and near-term market opportunities. The updated full year consolidated 2026 outlook is: Raising full year Orders to exceed $2.5 billion, compared to previous outlook of at least $2.0 billion Raising full year free cash flow conversion to approximately 100 percent of Adjusted EBITDA, compared to previous outlook of at least 55 percent Maintaining full year Revenue outlook of between $1.300 billion and $1.375 billion Maintaining full year Adjusted EBITDA outlook of between $200 million and $225 million “As we work to conclude another banner year at CECO, we expect to deliver results at or above the full year guidance we have raised multiple times throughout the year, including the most recent increase in August. The projects in our backlog remain largely on schedule, and our sales pipeline continues to expand in both size and quantity. While we anticipate additional orders to be realized in power markets, I would emphasize that demand for our environmental, industrial and thermal solutions remain equally robust and often are realized later in the project supply chain cycle and generally represent funded, active projects rather than speculative demand,” added Gleason. Introducing 2027 Full Year Outlook The Company introduces its 2027 full year outlook with revenue expected to be between $2.1 billion and $2.3 billion, representing growth of more than 60 percent at the midpoint compared with the midpoint of the Company’s 2026 revenue outlook, and Adjusted EBITDA between $475 million and $525 million, representing growth of more than 130 percent at the midpoint compared with the midpoint of the Company’s 2026 Adjusted EBITDA outlook. The Company expects full year free cash flow of between 50 percent and 60 percent of Adjusted EBITDA. Mr. Gleason concluded, “CECO continues to transform and drive high-performance thanks to our incredible teams executing at high levels and generating incredible returns on our growth and operating improvement investments. Our full year 2027 outlook highlights further growth and scale. Since introducing our annual guidance process back in Q3 2021, we have a solid track record of meeting or exceeding our outlook as we incorporate significant backlog and pipeline insights, conservative assumptions on inflation and key market dynamics. The combination of our proven process and consistent track record, along with our visibility into our backlog and sales pipeline, gives us high confidence that 2027 will be another rewarding year for all our stakeholders.” EARNINGS CONFERENCE CALL A conference call is scheduled for today at 8:30 a.m. ET to discuss the third quarter 2026 financial results. ABOUT CECO ENVIRONMENTAL CECO Environmental is a leading environmentally focused, diversified industrial company, serving the broad landscape of industrial air, industrial water and energy transition markets globally providing innovative solutions and application expertise. CECO helps companies grow their businesses with safe, clean, and more efficient solutions that help protect people, the environment and industrial equipment.  CECO solutions improve air and water quality, optimize emissions management, and increase energy efficiency for highly-engineered applications in power generation, midstream and downstream hydrocarbon processing and transport, electric vehicle production, polysilicon fabrication, semiconductor and electronics, battery production and recycling, specialty metals and steel production, beverage can production, and water/wastewater treatment and a wide range of other industrial end markets. CECO is listed on Texas Stock Exchange (TXSE) under the ticker symbol "CECO." Incorporated in 1966, CECO’s global headquarters is in Addison, Texas. A "non-GAAP financial measure" is a numerical measure of a company's historical or future financial performance, financial position, or cash flows that includes or excludes amounts from the most directly comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP basic and diluted earnings per share, Adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, and adjusted free cash flow as presented in the financial data included in this press release, have been adjusted to exclude the effects of acquisition and integration expenses; divestiture gains and expenses; amortization expenses for acquisition-related intangible assets; earn-out expenses (income); restructuring expenses; executive transition expenses; asbestos and other legal matter expenses; foreign currency remeasurement; and the associated tax benefit or cost of these items. Management believes that these items are not necessarily indicative of the Company’s ongoing operations and their exclusion provides individuals with additional information to better compare the Company's results over multiple periods. Management utilizes this information to evaluate its ongoing financial performance. Our financial statements may continue to be affected by items similar to those excluded in the non-GAAP adjustments described above, and exclusion of these items from our non-GAAP financial measures should not be construed as an inference that all such costs are unusual or infrequent. Non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP operating margin, Adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, and adjusted free cash flow are not calculated in accordance with GAAP, and should be considered supplemental to, and not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Non-GAAP financial measures have limitations in that they do not reflect all of the costs associated with the operations of our business as determined in accordance with GAAP. As a result, you should not consider these measures in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of CECO’s results as reported under GAAP. Additionally, CECO cautions investors that non-GAAP financial measures used by the Company may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. In accordance with the requirements of Regulation G issued by the Securities and Exchange Commission, non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP basic and diluted earnings per share, Adjusted EBITDA, free cash flow, and adjusted free cash flow stated in the tables above are reconciled to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. Forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures, including Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow included in the Company’s 2026 and 2027 outlook, were not reconciled to the comparable GAAP financial measures because the reconciliation could not be performed without unreasonable efforts.
