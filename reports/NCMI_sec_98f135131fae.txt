Reports Results for Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Completed acquisition of Captivate, creating a combined network of approximately 48,000 digital screens across movie theaters, office buildings and residential properties Raised 2026 operational transformation savings target to more than $7.5 million, reflecting progress on cost-savings initiatives and operational efficiencies Provided fourth quarter 2026 revenue outlook of $117.0 million to $124.0 million Announced CEO Tom Lesinski to retire as part of planned leadership transition Centennial, Colo. - November 10, 2026 - National CineMedia, Inc. (NASDAQ: NCMI) (the “Company” or “NCM”), the operator of a leading premium video and digital out-of-home advertising platform in the U.S., and managing member of National CineMedia, LLC (NCM LLC), NCM Holdings, LLC (NCM Holdings) and Captivate Holdings, LLC (Captivate), today announced its consolidated results for the fiscal third quarter ended October 1, 2026. “With Captivate now part of NCM, we have significantly expanded our reach, diversified our advertising offerings and created new opportunities to connect brands with highly engaged audiences where they work, live and play,” said Tom Lesinski, Chief Executive Officer of National CineMedia, Inc. “We are encouraged by early fourth-quarter trends across both networks, supported by a strong holiday theatrical slate and growing demand for Captivate’s office and residential inventory. Looking ahead, we remain focused on successfully integrating Captivate, realizing acquisition synergies and reducing leverage to drive sustainable growth and long-term value for our shareholders. As I prepare to retire, I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together and confident in NCM's future” Q3 2026 Results Total revenue for the third quarter ended October 1, 2026 decreased 3.2% to $61.4 million as compared to $63.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Operating loss increased to $21.6 million for the third quarter of 2026 from $1.8 million for the third quarter of 2025. About National CineMedia, Inc. National CineMedia, Inc. (NCM, NASDAQ:NCMI ) is a leading premium video and digital out-of-home advertising platform in the U.S. With unparalleled reach and scale, NCM, Inc.'s national network connects advertisers, through its two brands NCM and Captivate, to hard-to-reach, young, diverse and affluent audiences utilizing the power of high attention environments, including theaters, elevators and building lobbies. A premium video, full-funnel marketing solution for advertisers, NCM, Inc. enhances advertisers’ ability to measure and drive results. NCM’s Noovie® Show is presented exclusively in 38 leading national and regional theater circuits including all three national chains, AMC Entertainment Inc. (NYSE:AMC), Cinemark Holdings, Inc. (NYSE:CNK) and Regal Entertainment Group (a subsidiary of Cineworld Group PLC). NCM’s advertising platform, including Spotlight and Captivate, consists of approximately 48,000 total digital screens across theaters, office buildings and residential properties in 185 Designated Market Areas® (all of the top 100). NCM is the managing member and owner of 100% of National CineMedia, LLC (NCM LLC) and Captivate Holdings, LLC (Captivate). For more information, visit www.ncm.com. The Company believes the presentation of this measure is relevant and useful for investors because it enables them to view performance in a manner similar to the method used by the Company’s management, helps improve their ability to understand the Company’s operating performance and makes it easier to compare the Company’s results with other companies that may have different depreciation and amortization policies, non-cash share-based compensation programs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in the Cineworld Proceeding and Chapter 11 Case, interest rates, debt levels or income tax rates.
