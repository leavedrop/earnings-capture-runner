In certain circumstances, the company contracts with providers to accept financial risk for a defined set of Medicare Advantage membership. Providers who are contracted in an Upside Only/Shared Savings arrangement may receive a portion of achieved surpluses when the actual cost of the medical services provided to patients assigned or attributed to their panel is less than the agreed upon medical expense targets.
