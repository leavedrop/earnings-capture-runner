MSG Knickerbockers Corp. Reports Fiscal 2027 First Quarter Results

November 6, 2026
  • Download (opens in new window)

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- MSG Knickerbockers Corp. (NYSE: MSGK) ("MSG Knicks" or the "Company") today reported financial results for the fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.

On October 26, 2026, the Company completed the spin-off of its New York Rangers ("Rangers") business. As a result of the timing of the spin-off, the Company’s fiscal 2027 first quarter consolidated results include the financial results of the Rangers business.

For the fiscal 2027 first quarter, the Company generated revenues of $45.5 million, an increase of $6.0 million, or 15%, as compared to the prior year period. In addition, the Company reported an operating loss of $25.5 million, an improvement of $2.0 million, or 7%, and adjusted operating loss of $20.0 million, an improvement of $0.8 million, or 4%, both as compared to the prior year period.(1)

In addition, subsequent to the end of the quarter, the New York Knicks ("Knicks") began their 2026-27 regular season at the Madison Square Garden Arena (“The Garden”) with a banner raising and ring celebration to commemorate the team winning the 2025-26 NBA Championship.

MSG Knickerbockers Corp. Executive Chairman and CEO James L. Dolan said, “With the completion of the spin-off of our Rangers business and a new Knicks season underway, our business is well-positioned to build on our positive operating momentum. We remain confident in the value of owning this storied franchise and our ability to generate long-term shareholder value.”

Summary of Financial Results
For the fiscal 2027 first quarter, revenues of $45.5 million increased $6.0 million, or 15%, as compared to the prior year period. This primarily reflected higher revenues from league distributions of $3.1 million, mainly due to an increase in certain league distributions unrelated to national media rights fees. In addition, food, beverage and merchandise sales increased $2.6 million as compared to the prior year period, primarily due to higher Knicks merchandise revenues driven by ongoing demand from the team winning the 2025-26 NBA Championship.

Direct operating expenses of $8.8 million increased $0.5 million, or 7%, as compared to the prior year period. This was primarily driven by (i) higher expenses associated with merchandise sales of $1.6 million, (ii) higher team personnel compensation of $1.3 million, and (iii) higher net provisions for certain team personnel transactions of $0.7 million, mostly offset by (iv) a $3.0 million decrease in net provisions for league revenue sharing expense (net of escrow and excluding playoffs) and NBA luxury tax.

Selling, general and administrative expenses of $60.8 million increased $3.0 million, or 5%, as compared to the prior year period. This increase was primarily driven by (i) expenses related to the spin-off transaction in the current year period of $3.6 million and (ii) higher other general and administrative expenses, partially offset by (iii) lower employee compensation and related benefits of $1.2 million.

Operating loss of $25.5 million improved $2.0 million, or 7%, and adjusted operating loss of $20.0 million improved $0.8 million, or 4%, both as compared to the prior year period, primarily due to higher revenues, partially offset by higher selling, general and administrative expenses.

About MSG Knickerbockers Corp.
MSG Knickerbockers Corp. is a leading professional sports company that owns and operates the New York Knicks (NBA), as well as a development league team, the Westchester Knicks (NBAGL). As an original franchise of the NBA, the Knicks have a rich history, winning three NBA Championships including the 2025-26 NBA Championship. More information is available at msgknicks.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
We define adjusted operating income (loss), which is a non-GAAP financial measure, as operating income (loss) excluding (i) depreciation, amortization and impairments of property and equipment, goodwill and other intangible assets, (ii) share-based compensation expense or benefit, (iii) restructuring charges or credits, (iv) gains or losses on sales or dispositions of businesses, (v) the impact of purchase accounting adjustments related to business acquisitions, and (vi) gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan. Because it is based upon operating income (loss), adjusted operating income (loss) also excludes interest expense (including cash interest expense) and other non-operating income and expense items. We believe that the exclusion of share-based compensation expense or benefit allows investors to better track the performance of our business without regard to the settlement of an obligation that is not expected to be made in cash. In addition, we believe that the exclusion of gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan provides investors with a clearer picture of the Company’s operating performance given that, in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan are recognized in operating income (loss) whereas gains and losses related to the remeasurement of the assets under the Company’s Executive Deferred Compensation Plan, which are equal to and therefore fully offset the gains and losses related to the remeasurement of liabilities, are recognized in miscellaneous income (expense), net, which is not reflected in operating income (loss).

We believe adjusted operating income (loss) is an appropriate measure for evaluating the operating performance of our Company. Adjusted operating income (loss) and similar measures with similar titles are common performance measures used by investors and analysts to analyze our performance. Internally, we use revenues and adjusted operating income (loss) as the most important indicators of our business performance, and evaluate management’s effectiveness with specific reference to these indicators. Adjusted operating income (loss) should be viewed as a supplement to and not a substitute for operating income (loss), net income (loss), cash flows from operating activities, and other measures of performance and/or liquidity presented in accordance with GAAP. Since adjusted operating income (loss) is not a measure of performance calculated in accordance with GAAP, this measure may not be comparable to similar measures with similar titles used by other companies. For a reconciliation of operating income (loss) to adjusted operating income (loss), please see page 5 of this earnings release.

The following is a description of the adjustments to operating loss in arriving at adjusted operating loss as described in this earnings release:
