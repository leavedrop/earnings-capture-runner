Montréal, Québec, November 11, 2026 – CGI (TSX : GIB.A) (NYSE : GIB)
Q4-F2026 results
“CGI’s fourth quarter performance reflects the strength of our client relationships, the relevance of our offerings and the operational excellence of our team,” said Tim Hurlebaus, President and Chief Executive Officer. “We continued to deliver revenue growth, earnings expansion and strong bookings which were driven by a 122%1 quarterly book-to-bill ratio for our AI-embedded managed services.”
“As we enter Fiscal 2027, we are well positioned strategically, operationally and financially to invest in driving both build and buy growth. Advanced AI at enterprise scale is now routinely part of client conversations and proposals, and we see growing client interest in practical applications of quantum computing. Our commitment is to remain a partner of choice for helping clients around the world apply these technologies with industry expertise, modern data foundations, cybersecurity and the ability to govern responsibly and efficiently.”
For the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2026, the Company reported revenue of $4.15 billion, representing a year-over-year growth of 3.3%. When excluding foreign currency variations, revenue grew by 2.5% year-over-year.
Earnings before income taxes were $628.3 million, up 21.7% year-over-year, for a margin of 15.2%, up 230 basis points compared to the same period last year. Recorded in the period were acquisition and related integration costs of $19.7 million.
Adjusted earnings before interest and taxes2 were $678.0 million, up 1.6% year-over-year, for a margin of 16.4%, down 20 basis points compared to the same period last year.
Net earnings were $460.7 million, up 20.8% compared to the same period last year, for a margin of 11.1%, up 160 basis points compared to the same period last year. Diluted earnings per share, as a result, were $2.24 compared to $1.72 last year, representing an increase of 30.2%.
Adjusted net earnings2 were $475.4 million, up 0.8% compared to the same period last year, for a margin of 11.5%, down 30 basis points compared to the same period last year. On the same basis, diluted earnings per share increased by 8.5% to $2.31 from $2.13 for the same period last year.
During the fourth quarter Fiscal 2026, we distributed $35.1 million back to our shareholders through the payment of a cash dividend.
Cash provided by operating activities was $551.9 million, representing 13.3% of revenue. On a trailing twelve month basis, cash provided by operating activities was $2.48 billion, representing 15.0% of revenue.
Bookings were $4.53 billion, representing a book-to-bill ratio of 109.2%. The book-to-bill ratio for the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026 was 105.6%.
As of September 30, 2026, the number of CGI consultants and professionals worldwide stood at approximately 94,000.
During the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2026, the Company invested $89.4 million back into its business, acquired businesses for an investment of $34.6 million net of cash acquired, and invested $166.7 million under its Normal Course Issuer Bid to purchase and cancel 1,650,700 Class A subordinate voting shares and to pay for 54,900 Class A subordinate voting shares, which were purchased but were neither paid nor cancelled as at June 30, 2026.

F2026 results

The Company reported revenue of $16.57 billion, representing a year-over-year growth of 4.2%. When excluding foreign currency variations, revenue grew by 2.2% year-over-year.
Earnings before income taxes were $2,479.7 million, up 10.6% year-over-year, for a margin of 15.0%, up 90 basis points compared to the previous fiscal year. Recorded in the period were acquisition and related integration costs of $104.0 million.
Adjusted earnings before interest and taxes1 were $2,706.4 million, up 3.7% year-over-year, for a margin of 16.3%, down 10 basis points compared to the previous fiscal year.
Net earnings were $1,812.6 million, up 9.3% compared to the previous fiscal year, for a margin of 10.9%, up 50 basis points compared to the previous fiscal year. Diluted earnings per share, as a result, were $8.58 compared to $7.35 last year, representing an increase of 16.7%.
Adjusted net earnings1 were $1,898.0 million, up 1.4% compared to the same period last year, for a margin of 11.5%, down 30 basis points compared to the previous fiscal year. On the same basis, diluted earnings per share increased by 8.2% to $8.98 from $8.30 for the same period last year.
During Fiscal 2026, we distributed $144.1 million back to our shareholders through the payment of cash dividends.
Cash provided by operating activities was $2.48 billion, an increase of 11.0% on a year-over-year basis, representing 15.0% of revenue.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s backlog reached $32.07 billion or 1.9 x annual revenue.
During Fiscal 2026, the Company invested $385.4 million back into its business, acquired businesses for an investment of $198.2 million net of cash acquired, and invested $1,553.1 million under its previous and current Normal Course Issuer Bid to purchase and cancel 14,164,627 Class A subordinate voting shares and to pay for 80,300 Class A subordinate voting shares, which were purchased but were neither paid nor cancelled as at September 30, 2025.
As at September 30, 2026, long-term debt and lease liabilities, including both their current and long-term portions, were $3.96 billion, down from $4.33 billion at the same time last year. This was mainly driven by the scheduled repayment in full in September 2026 of senior unsecured notes in the amount of $834.9 million (US$600.0 million), partially offset by the issuance of senior unsecured notes for net proceeds of $498.6 million in September 2026. As of the same date, net debt2 stood at $3.42 billion, down from $3.45 billion at the same time last year. The net debt-to-capitalization ratio2 was 24.7% at the end of September 2026, compared to 25.1% last year.

About CGI
Founded in 1976, CGI is among the largest independent IT and business consulting services firms in the world. With 94,000 consultants and professionals across the globe, CGI delivers an end-to-end portfolio of capabilities, from strategic IT and business consulting to systems integration, managed IT and business process services and intellectual property solutions. CGI works with clients through a local relationship model complemented by a global delivery network that helps clients digitally transform their organizations and accelerate results. CGI Fiscal 2026 reported revenue is $16.57 billion and CGI shares are listed on the TSX (GIB.A) and the NYSE (GIB). Learn more at cgi.com.
