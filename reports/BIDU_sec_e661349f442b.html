Baidu Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

BEIJING, China, November 16, 2026 – Baidu, Inc. (NASDAQ: BIDU and HKEX: 9888 (HKD Counter) and 89888 (RMB Counter)), (“Baidu” or the “Company”), a leading AI company with strong Internet foundation, today announced its unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Our long-term investments in full-stack AI are translating into meaningful growth at scale. This quarter, AI Cloud Infra emerged as a major driver of Baidu General Business revenue expansion, and Baidu Core AI-powered Business1 accounted for 53% of Baidu General Business revenue, further reinforcing AI’s position at the core of our business. We also continued to expand use cases, strengthen capabilities, and improve user experience across our AI applications portfolio,” said Robin Li, Co-founder and CEO of Baidu. “We are pleased to see our AI-first strategy yielding tangible results even as we navigate headwinds in online marketing. AI is playing an increasingly important role in shaping Baidu’s future, and we remain committed to investing decisively to capture long-term growth opportunities and solidify our competitive edge.”

“In Q3, Baidu General Business revenue returned to year-over-year growth, increasing by 1%. AI Cloud Infra revenue1 grew 94% year over year, helping to partially offset weakness in online marketing and supporting the overall top-line recovery. AI Cloud Infra is becoming a new growth engine for Baidu, with an increasing contribution to both revenue and profit. Operating cash flow for Baidu also remained positive in the third quarter, reaching RMB 2.8 billion, underscoring our solid cash generation and financial health,” said Haijian He, CFO of Baidu. “We will maintain a disciplined approach to capital allocation with a clear focus on investment returns as we continue to invest in AI. “

Baidu Core AI-powered Business

AI Cloud Infra

 

  •  

Revenue from AI Cloud Infra was RMB 8.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 94% year over year. Within AI Cloud Infra, revenue from GPU Cloud2 increased by 191% year over year and 43% quarter over quarter in the third quarter of 2026.

 

  •  

In the third quarter of 2026, AI Cloud Infra continued to experience strong demand, with a more diversified customer base, increased customer prepayments, and a continued shift toward longer-term contracts.

Robotaxi
