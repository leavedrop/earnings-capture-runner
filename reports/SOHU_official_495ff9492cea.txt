Press Release PDF Version << Back SOHU.COM REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS November 9, 2026 at 12:00 AM EST BEIJING , Nov. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sohu.com Limited (NASDAQ: SOHU) ("Sohu" or the "Company"), a leading Chinese online media platform and game business group, today reported unaudited financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 . Third Quarter Highlights [1] Total revenues were US$133 million , down 26% year-over-year and 2% quarter-over-quarter. Marketing services revenues were US$15 million , up 10% year-over-year and down 2% quarter-over-quarter. Online game revenues were US$114 million , down 30% year-over-year and 2% quarter-over-quarter. GAAP net loss attributable to Sohu.com Limited was US$15 million , compared with net income of US$9 million in the third quarter of 2025 and net income of US$0.2 million in the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP [2] net loss attributable to Sohu.com Limited was US$15 million , compared with net income of US$9 million in the third quarter of 2025 and net income of US$0.5 million in the second quarter of 2026. Dr. Charles Zhang , Chairman and CEO of Sohu.com Limited , commented, "In the third quarter of 2026, our marketing services revenues hit the high end of our guidance, while both our online game revenues and our bottom-line performance were in line with our expectations. For the Sohu social media platform, we continued to strengthen its social features through numerous iconic events, fostering genuine connections among users and boosting active user interactions across multiple verticals. We also kept leveraging our integrated product matrix with innovative and customized marketing solutions to unlock monetization potential. For our online games, we continued to introduce fresh content to sustain player interest while offering tailored gameplay experiences to meet evolving player needs." Third Quarter Financial Results Revenues Total revenues were US$133 million , down 26% year-over-year and 2% quarter-over-quarter. Marketing services revenues were US$15 million , up 10% year-over-year and down 2% quarter-over-quarter. Online game revenues were US$114 million , down 30% year-over-year and 2% quarter-over-quarter. Cost of Revenues Both GAAP and non-GAAP total cost of revenues were US$32 million , down 9% year-over-year and up 9% quarter-over-quarter. Both GAAP and non-GAAP cost of marketing services revenues were US$15 million , up 23% year-over-year and 11% quarter-over-quarter. Both GAAP and non-GAAP cost of online game revenues were US$14 million , down 33% year-over-year and up 2% quarter-over-quarter. Non-GAAP net loss attributable to Sohu.com Limited was US$15 million , or a net loss of US$0.59 per fully-diluted ADS, compared with net income of US$9 million in the third quarter of 2025 and net income of US$0.5 million in the second quarter of 2026. Liquidity and Capital Resources As of September 30, 2026 , cash and cash equivalents, short-term investments and long-term time deposits totaled approximately US$1.2 billion . Supplementary Information for Changyou Results [3] Third Quarter 2026 Operating Results For PC games, total average monthly active user accounts [4] (MAU) were 2.6 million, a decrease of 4% year-over-year and flat quarter-over-quarter. Total quarterly aggregate active paying accounts [5] (APA) were 1.0 million, a decrease of 8% year-over-year and an increase of 4% quarter-over-quarter. The year-over-year decrease in APA was mainly due to the natural decline of Changyou's older games, including Tian Long Ba Bu ("TLBB") PC, and to the natural decline of TLBB: Return, which was launched during the third quarter of 2025. For mobile games, total average MAU were 1.4 million, a decrease of 24% year-over-year and 16% quarter-over-quarter. Total quarterly APA were 0.2 million, a decrease of 25% year-over-year and 4% quarter-over-quarter. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter decreases in MAU and APA were mainly due to the natural decline of Changyou's older games. Third Quarter 2026 Unaudited Financial Results Total revenues were US$115 million , a decrease of 30% year-over-year and 2% quarter-over-quarter. Online game revenues were US$114 million , a decrease of 30% year-over-year and 2% quarter-over-quarter. For online game revenues, the year-over-year decrease was mainly due to a natural decline from a high comparison base driven by content updates released for TLBB PC and the launch of TLBB: Return in the third quarter of 2025. Both GAAP and non-GAAP total cost of revenues were US$14 million , a decrease of 33% year-over-year and an increase of 2% quarter-over-quarter. Both GAAP and non-GAAP operating expenses were US$42 million , a decrease of 23% year-over-year and 12% quarter-over-quarter. The year-over-year decrease was mainly due to a decrease in salary and benefits expenses, as well as a decrease in marketing and promotional spending for our online games. The quarter-over-quarter decrease was mainly due to a decrease in licensing fees related to product development. GAAP operating profit was US$58 million , compared with US$87 million for the third quarter of 2025 and US$55 million for the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP operating profit was US$59 million , compared with US$88 million for the third quarter of 2025 and US$56 million for the second quarter of 2026. Non-GAAP Disclosure To supplement the unaudited consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America ("GAAP"), Sohu's management uses non-GAAP measures of operating profit/(loss), net income/(loss), net income/(loss) attributable to Sohu.com Limited and diluted net income/(loss) attributable to Sohu.com Limited per ADS, which are adjusted from results based on GAAP to exclude the impact of share-based compensation expense. These measures should be considered in addition to results prepared in accordance with GAAP, but should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, GAAP results. Sohu's management believes excluding share-based compensation expense from the Company's non-GAAP financial measures is useful for itself and investors. Further, the impact of share-based compensation expense could not be anticipated by management and business line leaders, and these expenses were not built into the annual budgets and quarterly forecasts that have been the basis for information Sohu provides to analysts and investors as guidance for future operating performance. As share-based compensation expense does not involve subsequent cash outflow and is not reflected in the cash flows at the equity transaction level, Sohu does not factor in its impact when evaluating and approving expenditures or when determining the allocation of its resources to its business segments. As a result, in general, the monthly financial results for internal reporting and any performance measures for commissions and bonuses are based on non-GAAP financial measures that exclude share-based compensation expense. The non-GAAP financial measures are provided to enhance investors' overall understanding of Sohu's current financial performance and prospects for the future. A limitation of using non-GAAP operating profit/(loss), net income/(loss), net income/(loss) attributable to Sohu.com Limited , and diluted net income/(loss) attributable to Sohu.com Limited per ADS excluding share-based compensation expense is that this expense has been and can be expected to continue to recur in Sohu's business. In order to mitigate these limitations Sohu has provided specific information regarding the GAAP amounts excluded from each non-GAAP measure. The accompanying tables include details on the reconciliation between the GAAP financial measures that are most directly comparable to the non-GAAP financial measures that have been presented. About Sohu Sohu.com Limited (NASDAQ: SOHU) was established by Dr. Charles Zhang , one of China's internet pioneers, in the 1990s. Sohu operates one of the leading Chinese online media platforms and also engages in the online game business in the Chinese mainland. Sohu has built one of the most comprehensive matrices of Chinese language web properties, consisting of Sohu News App, Sohu Video App, the mobile portal m.sohu.com , the PC portal www.sohu.com , and the online games platform www.changyou.com/en/ . As a mainstream media platform with social features, Sohu is indispensable to the daily life of millions of Chinese, providing to a vast number of users a network of web properties and community based products, which offer a broad array of content, such as news and information, in the form of text, picture, video, and live broadcasting. Sohu also attracts users to actively engage in content generation and distribution, and actively interact with each other on the platform. Sohu's online game business is conducted by its subsidiary Changyou, which develops and operates a diverse portfolio of PC and mobile games, such as the well-known TLBB PC and Legacy TLBB Mobile.
