Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2026 Summary: Reflecting as reported GAAP performance from continuing operations, fourth quarter revenue of $3.9 billion decreased 7% over the prior year, operating income decreased 5% to $226 million , net income declined 3% to $128 million and diluted earnings per share was unchanged at $0.99 . For the full year, revenue of $15.1 billion reflected a 6% decrease over the prior year, operating income decreased 40% to $620 million , net income decreased 42% to $369 million and diluted earnings per share decreased 41% to $2.84 . Results for the full year included the $337 million pre-tax charge resulting from a higher projected cost to complete a Construction Management project reported in the third quarter results. Net service revenue 2 increased by 3% in the fourth quarter after adjusting for the impact of fewer working days versus the prior year, driven by 6% growth in the design business. Fourth quarter adjusted 1 EBITDA 5 and adjusted 1 EPS increased by 9% and 20%, respectively. Both the fourth quarter segment adjusted 1 operating margin 4 and the adjusted EBITDA margin 6 set new quarterly records, increasing by 110 basis points to 18.2% and by 170 basis points to 19.2%, respectively. Total backlog 8 increased by 13% to a record high, driven by record total wins of $13.4 billion in the year and a 1.3 book-to-burn ratio 9 across the enterprise. Total wins in the fourth quarter were $2.9 billion , resulting in a 1.1 book-to-burn ratio across the enterprise. The design pipeline increased again to a new all-time high, including growth in both early-stage and late-stage pursuits, as well as the fifth consecutive quarter of double-digit pipeline growth in the Americas . Cash Flow and Capital Allocation Operating cash flow of $366 million contributed to free cash flow 7 of $274 million in the fourth quarter, resulting in full year free cash flow of $344 million , which was ahead of the Company’s prior guidance. Underlying cash flow remains strong and the Company continues to expect to deliver on its long-term 100%+ free cash flow conversion target once the Construction Management project headwinds subside, which is anticipated by end of the first half of fiscal 2027, consistent with prior expectations. Reflecting the Company’s strong balance sheet and expectation for continued strong cash flow, the Board of Directors approved a 10% increase to the quarterly dividend to $0.34 per share. The Company has grown its per share dividend at an 18% CAGR since its initiation, delivering on its commitment to increase the value of its per share dividend by a double-digit percentage annually. The increased dividend as declared by the Board will be reflected in the Company’s next dividend payment on January 22, 2027 , to stockholders of record on January 6, 2027 . The Company remains committed to its returns-based capital allocation policy, which in the near-term will be prioritized towards its organic growth investments and its quarterly dividend program. The Company maintains a strong balance sheet with net leverage 10 of 1.3x. Fiscal 2027 Financial Guidance AECOM initiated financial guidance for fiscal 2027 that contemplates the benefits from the Company’s record backlog and pipeline, strong end market trends, and expected margin expansion to a new all-time high: Organic NSR 2 growth of between 4% and 6%, excluding the Construction Management project charge in fiscal 2026. Both a segment adjusted 1 operating margin 4 and an adjusted EBITDA margin 6 of 17.6%. Adjusted 1 EPS of between $6.15 and $6.35 and adjusted 1 EBITDA 5 of between $1,350 and $1,390 million . Free cash flow 7 of approximately $300 million , which includes a continued expectation for approximately $500 million of cash use related to the two Construction Management projects in fiscal 2027. An average fully diluted share count of 130 million, which does not include any benefits from potential capital allocation actions, such as potential repurchases. An adjusted 1 effective tax rate of approximately 22%. Adjusted 1 net interest expense of approximately $170 million . General and administrative expenses of approximately $160 million . Business Segments Americas Revenue in the fourth quarter was $3.0 billion , a 9% decrease from the prior year, and full year revenue was $11.5 billion , an 8% decrease over the prior year, both which were driven primarily by declines in the Construction Management business. Net service revenue 2 in the fourth quarter was $1.1 billion , a 1% increase from the prior year after adjusting for the impact of fewer working days, driven by 6% growth in the design business. Full year net service revenue 2 was $4.3 billion , a 3% increase from the prior year after adjusting for the impact of fewer working days, which included 7% growth in the design business. Fourth quarter operating income decreased by 7% over the prior year to $227 million and decreased by 41% for the full year to $531 million . On an adjusted 1 basis, fourth quarter operating income decreased by 3% to $236 million , driven primarily by the impact of fewer working days in the quarter versus the prior year period, and decreased by 37% to $566 million for the full year. The fourth quarter adjusted 1 operating margin 4 on net service revenue increased 20 basis points to 20.6%. Backlog 8 in the Americas segment grew by 10% to a new record high, driven by a 1.2 book-to-burn ratio 9 in the quarter and strong wins across each of the Company’s key end markets. International Revenue in the fourth quarter was $928 million , a 1% decrease from the prior year. Full year revenue was $3.6 billion , effectively unchanged from the prior year. Net service revenue 2 in the fourth quarter was $775 million , a 5% increase from the prior year after adjusting for the impact of fewer working days versus the prior year, driven by growth in the U.K . and Australia , as well as a return to growth in the Middle East . Full year net service revenue 2 was $3.1 billion , a 1% increase from the prior year after adjusting for the impact of fewer working days versus the prior year. Fourth quarter operating income increased by 18% over the prior year to $109 million and increased by 7% to $371 million for the full year. On an adjusted 1 basis, operating income increased by 24% to $114 million in the fourth quarter and increased by 14% to $393 million for the full year. The fourth quarter adjusted 1 operating margin 4 on net service revenue increased by 270 basis points to 14.8%, which set a new quarterly record for the International segment, driven by the benefits from growth in higher-margin markets and strong execution. The full year adjusted operating margin on net service revenue increased by 140 basis points to 12.8%. Backlog 8 in the International segment grew 21% over the prior year and remains near a record high, supported by strong wins across the business over the course of the year and a 1.4 book-to-burn ratio 9 for the year, highlighted by an at least 1.3 book-to-burn ratio in each of its Europe , Australia and Middle East businesses. Tax Rate The effective tax rate was 24.6% in the fourth quarter and 17.0% for the full year. On an adjusted 1 basis, the effective tax rate was 24.9% in the fourth quarter and 19.7% for the full year. The adjusted tax rate was derived by re-computing the quarterly effective tax rate on adjusted net income 11 . The adjusted tax expense differs from the GAAP tax expense based on the taxability or deductibility and tax rate applied to each of the adjustments. Non-GAAP Financial Information This communication contains financial information calculated other than in accordance with U.S . generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). The Company believes that non-GAAP financial measures such as adjusted EPS, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted net/operating income, segment adjusted operating margin, adjusted tax rate, net service revenue and free cash flow provide a meaningful perspective on its business results as the Company utilizes this information to evaluate and manage the business. We use adjusted operating income, adjusted net income, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA margin, and adjusted EPS to exclude the impact of certain items, such as amortization expense and taxes to aid investors in better understanding our core performance results. We use free cash flow to present the cash generated from operations after capital expenditures to maintain our business. We present net service revenue (NSR) to exclude pass-through subcontractor costs from revenue to provide investors with a better understanding of our operational performance. We present segment adjusted operating margin to reflect segment operating performance of our Americas and International segments, excluding AECOM Capital . We present adjusted tax rate to reflect the tax rate on adjusted earnings. We also use constant-currency growth rates where appropriate, which are calculated by conforming the current period results to the comparable period exchange rates. Our non-GAAP disclosure has limitations as an analytical tool, should not be viewed as a substitute for financial information determined in accordance with GAAP, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP, nor is it necessarily comparable to non-GAAP performance measures that may be presented by other companies. A reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures is found in the Regulation G Information tables at the back of this communication. The Company is unable to reconcile certain of its non-GAAP financial guidance and long-term financial targets due to uncertainties in these non-operating items as well as other adjustments to net income. The Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of its guidance for NSR to GAAP revenue because it is unable to predict with reasonable certainty its pass-through revenue.
