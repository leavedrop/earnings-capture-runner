National CineMedia, Inc. Reports Results for Fiscal Third Quarter 2026

November 10, 2026
PDF Version

Completed acquisition of Captivate, creating a combined network of approximately 48,000 digital screens across movie theaters, office buildings and residential properties

Raised 2026 operational transformation savings target to more than $7.5 million, reflecting progress on cost-savings initiatives and operational efficiencies

Provided fourth quarter 2026 revenue outlook of $117.0 million to $124.0 million

Announced CEO Tom Lesinski to retire as part of planned leadership transition

CENTENNIAL, Colo.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Nov. 10, 2026-- National CineMedia, Inc. (NASDAQ: NCMI) (the “Company” or “NCM”), the operator of a leading premium video and digital out-of-home advertising platform in the U.S., and managing member of National CineMedia, LLC (NCM LLC), NCM Holdings, LLC (NCM Holdings), and Captivate Holdings, LLC (Captivate), today announced its consolidated results for the fiscal third quarter ended October 1, 2026.

“With Captivate now part of NCM, we have significantly expanded our reach, diversified our advertising offerings, and created new opportunities to connect brands with highly engaged audiences where they work, live, and play,” said Tom Lesinski, Chief Executive Officer of National CineMedia, Inc. “We are encouraged by early fourth-quarter trends across both networks, supported by a strong holiday theatrical slate and growing demand for Captivate’s office and residential inventory. Looking ahead, we remain focused on successfully integrating Captivate, realizing acquisition synergies, and reducing leverage to drive sustainable growth and long-term value for our shareholders. As I prepare to retire, I am incredibly proud of what we have accomplished together and confident in NCM’s future.”

Q3 2026 Results

Total revenue for the third quarter ended October 1, 2026 decreased 3.2% to $61.4 million as compared to $63.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Operating loss increased to $21.6 million for the third quarter of 2026 from $1.8 million for the third quarter of 2025. Net loss increased to $17.2 million, or $0.18 net loss per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026 from net income of $1.6 million, or $0.02 net income per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted OIBDA, a non-GAAP measure, decreased to $3.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 from $10.2 million for the third quarter of 2025, as adjusted to exclude depreciation, amortization, non-cash share-based payment costs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in Regal's Chapter 11 case (the “Cineworld Proceeding”) and NCM LLC's Chapter 11 case (“Chapter 11 Case”), each as previously reported and described in the Company’s public filings made with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”). As adjusted to exclude the aforementioned items and gain on remeasurement of the payable to ESA Parties under the tax receivable agreement, net income per diluted share for the quarter ended October 1, 2026 is $0.33 compared to net income per diluted share for the quarter ended September 25, 2025 of $0.00. Adjusted OIBDA, adjusted net loss and adjusted net loss per share are non-GAAP measures. See the tables at the end of this release for the reconciliations to the closest GAAP basis measurements. Captivate results were consolidated into the Company's financial statements as of September 18, 2026, the date of the acquisition.

Total revenue for the nine months ended October 1, 2026 increased 2.5% to $153.8 million as compared to $150.0 million for the nine months ended September 25, 2025. Operating loss increased to $61.3 million for the nine months ended October 1, 2026 from $37.7 million for the nine months ended September 25, 2025. Net loss increased to $55.8 million, or $0.60 net loss per diluted share, for the nine months ended October 1, 2026 from $39.9 million, or $0.42 net loss per diluted share, for the nine months ended September 25, 2025. Adjusted OIBDA, a non-GAAP measure, decreased to negative $5.2 million for the nine months ended October 1, 2026 from positive $1.9 million for the nine months ended September 25, 2025, as adjusted to exclude depreciation, amortization, non-cash share-based payment costs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in the Cineworld Proceeding and the Chapter 11 Case, each as previously reported and described in the Company’s public filings made with the SEC. As adjusted to exclude the aforementioned items and (gain) loss on remeasurement of the payable to ESA Parties under the tax receivable agreement, net income per diluted share for the nine months ended October 1, 2026, is $0.00 compared to net loss per diluted share for the nine months ended September 25, 2025 of $0.37. Adjusted OIBDA, adjusted net loss and adjusted net loss per share are non-GAAP measures. See the tables at the end of this release for the reconciliations to the closest GAAP basis measurements. Captivate results were consolidated into the Company's financial statements as of September 18, 2026, the date of the acquisition.

Leadership Transition

Tom Lesinski will retire as Chief Executive Officer as part of a transition plan, with an anticipated departure of December 31, 2026. The Board of Directors has retained Korn Ferry to assist with a comprehensive search for the Company’s next Chief Executive Officer. Additional details are available in the Company’s press release issued today.

Outlook

For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Company expects to earn total revenue of $117.0 million to $124.0 million and Adjusted OIBDA in the range of $42.0 million to $48.0 million.

About National CineMedia, Inc.

National CineMedia, Inc. (NCM, NASDAQ:NCMI) is a leading premium video and digital out-of-home advertising platform in the U.S. With unparalleled reach and scale, NCM, Inc.'s national network connects advertisers, through its two brands NCM and Captivate, to hard-to-reach, young, diverse and affluent audiences utilizing the power of high attention environments, including theaters, elevators and building lobbies. A premium video, full-funnel marketing solution for advertisers, NCM, Inc. enhances advertisers’ ability to measure and drive results. NCM’s Noovie® Show is presented exclusively in 38 leading national and regional theater circuits including all three national chains, AMC Entertainment Inc. (NYSE:AMC), Cinemark Holdings, Inc. (NYSE:CNK) and Regal Entertainment Group (a subsidiary of Cineworld Group PLC). NCM’s advertising platform, including Spotlight and Captivate, consists of approximately 48,000 total digital screens across theaters, office buildings and residential properties in 185 Designated Market Areas® (all of the top 100). NCM is the managing member and owner of 100% of National CineMedia, LLC (NCM LLC) and Captivate Holdings, LLC (Captivate). For more information, visit www.ncm.com.

Adjusted OIBDA and Adjusted OIBDA Margin

Adjusted Operating Income Before Depreciation and Amortization (“Adjusted OIBDA”) and Adjusted OIBDA margin are not financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP in the United States.

Adjusted OIBDA represents operating income before depreciation and amortization expense adjusted to also exclude non-cash share-based compensation costs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in Regal’s Chapter 11 case (the “Cineworld Proceeding”) and NCM LLC's Chapter 11 Case (the “Chapter 11 Case”). Our management uses this non-GAAP financial measure to evaluate operating performance, to forecast future results and as a basis for compensation. The Company believes this is an important supplemental measure of operating performance because it eliminates items that have less bearing on its operating performance and highlight trends in its core business that may not otherwise be apparent when relying solely on GAAP financial measures. The Company believes the presentation of this measure is relevant and useful for investors because it enables them to view performance in a manner similar to the method used by the Company’s management, helps improve their ability to understand the Company’s operating performance and makes it easier to compare the Company’s results with other companies that may have different depreciation and amortization policies, non-cash share-based compensation programs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in the Cineworld Proceeding and Chapter 11 Case, interest rates, debt levels or income tax rates.

Adjusted OIBDA margin is calculated by dividing Adjusted OIBDA by total revenue. Our management uses this non-GAAP financial measure to evaluate operating performance, to forecast future results and as a basis for compensation. The Company believes this is an important supplemental measure of operating performance because it eliminates items that have less bearing on its operating performance and highlight trends in its core business that may not otherwise be apparent when relying solely on GAAP financial measures. The Company believes the presentation of this measure is relevant and useful for investors because it enables them to view performance in a manner similar to the method used by the Company’s management, helps improve their ability to understand the Company’s operating performance and makes it easier to compare the Company’s results with other companies that may have different depreciation and amortization policies, non-cash share-based compensation programs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in the Cineworld Proceeding and Chapter 11 Case, interest rates, debt levels or income tax rates.

A limitation of both of these measures, however, is that they exclude depreciation and amortization, which represent a proxy for the periodic costs of certain capitalized tangible and intangible assets used in generating revenues in NCM LLC’s business. In addition, Adjusted OIBDA and Adjusted OIBDA margin have the limitation of not reflecting the effect of the Company’s non-cash share-based compensation costs, workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs and advisor fees related to involvement in the Cineworld Proceeding and the Chapter 11 Case. Adjusted OIBDA should not be regarded as an alternative to operating income, net income or as indicators of operating performance, nor should it be considered in isolation of, or as substitutes for financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. The Company believes that operating income is the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure to Adjusted OIBDA, and operating margin is the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure to Adjusted OIBDA margin. Because not all companies use identical calculations, these non-GAAP presentations may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies, or calculations in NCM LLC’s debt agreement.

The Company has not provided a reconciliation of the forward-looking non-GAAP Adjusted OIBDA measure to forward-looking GAAP operating income due to the inability to predict the amount and timing of impacts outside of the Company’s control on certain items, including the timing of revenue and charges reflected in our reconciliation of historic numbers, the amount of which, based on historical experience, could be significant and are difficult to reasonably predict. Accordingly, a reconciliation of this non-GAAP measure is not available without unreasonable effort.

Adjusted Net Income (Loss) and Income (Loss) per Share

Adjusted net income (loss) and adjusted net income (loss) per share are not financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP in the United States. Adjusted net income (loss) and adjusted net income (loss) per share are calculated using reported net (loss) income and net (loss) income per share and exclude workforce and system transformation costs, satellite transition costs, acquisition and integration costs, advisor fees related to the Cineworld Proceeding and the Chapter 11 Case and loss on re-measurement of the payable to ESA Parties under the tax receivable agreement. Our management use these non-GAAP financial measures as an additional tool to evaluate operating performance. The Company believes these are important supplemental measures of operating performance because they eliminate items that have less bearing on its operating performance and so highlight trends in its core business that may not otherwise be apparent when relying solely on GAAP financial measures. The Company believes the presentation of these measures is relevant and useful for investors because it enables them to view performance in a manner similar to a method used by the Company’s management and helps improve their ability to understand the Company’s operating performance. Adjusted net income (loss) and adjusted net income (loss) per share should not be regarded as alternatives to net (loss) income and net (loss) income per share or as indicators of operating performance, nor should they be considered in isolation of, or as substitutes for financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. The Company believes that net (loss) income and net (loss) income per share are the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures. Because not all companies use identical calculations, these presentations may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.
