EXHIBIT 99.1

Brookfield Corporation Reports 16% Increase in Earnings

Record LTM Distributable Earnings Before Realizations of $5.8 Billion

Net Income of $1.9 Billion Reported for the Quarter

$300 Million of Shares Repurchased Since Last Report, Approximately $900 Million Repurchased Year-to-Date

BROOKFIELD, NEWS, Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Brookfield Corporation (NYSE: BN, TSX: BN) announced strong financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Nick Goodman, President of Brookfield Corporation, said, “We delivered strong financial results in the third quarter. Our asset management business raised $25 billion, wealth solutions delivered 22% earnings growth, and our operating businesses continued to generate resilient cash flows.”

Goodman added, “The current macroeconomic environment plays to our strengths. Our focus on real assets with inflation-linked revenues, pricing power, and durable cash flows positions us well for continued growth. We continue to reinvest our excess cash flows to compound capital. This included share repurchases of over $300 million since we last reported, bringing total share repurchases to almost $900 million so far this year.”

Operating Results

Distributable earnings (“DE”) before realizations per share increased by 14% and total DE, including realizations, increased by 16% per share over the prior-year quarter.

Total consolidated net income was $1.9 billion for the quarter and $5.3 billion for the last twelve months. Distributable earnings before realizations were $1.5 billion ($0.64/share) for the quarter and $5.8 billion ($2.47/share) for the last twelve months.

Asset Management delivered strong results, with fee-related earnings increasing 15% compared to the prior-year quarter. Growing investor demand for long-duration, essential-service and inflation-protected assets supported strong fundraising momentum, with $25 billion of capital raised during the quarter. Fee-bearing capital increased to $682 billion at quarter end.

Wealth Solutions continued to scale, with distributable earnings increasing 22% compared to the prior-year quarter, supported by continued organic growth, strong investment performance, and the expansion of our global insurance platform with the acquisition of Just Group in the prior quarter, bringing total insurance assets to nearly $200 billion.

Operating Businesses continued to generate stable and resilient cash flows, supported by strong operating fundamentals and the contracted and inflation-linked revenues of our underlying assets.

We realized $222 million of carried interest, net, into income during the quarter, bringing total realized carried interest, net, to $588 million over the last twelve months, reflecting continued progress in converting accumulated carried interest into earnings. Total distributable earnings were $1.7 billion ($0.73/share) for the quarter and $6.4 billion ($2.72/share) for the last twelve months.

Operating Highlights

Asset Management

  • DE was $755 million ($0.32/share) in the quarter and $3.0 billion ($1.28/share) for the last twelve months.
  • Fundraising through the third quarter totaled $123 billion, including $25 billion raised during the quarter, reflecting strong demand across our flagship and complementary strategies, including a record $1.2 billion raised for our infrastructure private wealth strategy and a record $1.5 billion for our super-core infrastructure strategy during the quarter.
  • Subsequent to quarter-end, we received $13 billion of additional commitments for our infrastructure flagship strategy, bringing total capital committed to date to $26 billion. We expect to finalize the first close in the coming weeks, positioning the fund to become, by far, the largest infrastructure fund ever raised globally.
  • Fee-bearing capital increased by 13% to $682 billion at quarter end, driving a 15% increase in fee-related earnings compared to the prior year quarter.

Wealth Solutions

  • DE was $511 million ($0.22/share) in the quarter and $1.9 billion ($0.79/share) for the last twelve months.
  • During the quarter, we originated $4 billion of retail and institutional annuity sales, supported by the expansion of our distribution platform in the U.S. through new broker-dealer channels. We continue to adopt a disciplined approach to organic growth with a primary focus on maintaining a return on our equity of 15%+ over the long term.
  • We deployed $13 billion of capital during the quarter, of which $8 billion was invested into real asset strategies. The balance primarily being the reallocation of cash and short-duration investments into higher-yielding U.S. Treasuries. Returns from real asset investments contributed to a 16 basis point increase in the total net yield of our investment portfolio (to 5.7%) compared to the prior quarter.
  • Our P&C business maintained strong underwriting performance, achieving a combined ratio of 99.6% during the quarter, further diversifying our liability profile and lowering our overall effective cost of funds.

Operating Businesses

  • DE was $467 million ($0.20/share) in the quarter and $1.6 billion ($0.70/share) for the last twelve months.
  • Cash distributions from our operating businesses continued to be supported by resilient operating earnings, underpinned by contracted, inflation-linked revenues and growing demand for the essential products and services our businesses provide.
  • Our energy business continues to advance strategic initiatives in nuclear power through Westinghouse, including a recently announced framework agreement between Westinghouse, the U.S. federal government, and Korea to accelerate nuclear development in the U.S., further strengthening the outlook for this business.
  • Operating fundamentals across our real estate portfolio remain strong as growing demand for high-quality assets, combined with constrained supply, continues to increase rents for premium assets. During the quarter, we completed 5.3 million square feet of office and retail leasing, with net rents across our balance sheet office portfolio 16% above expiring levels.

Earnings from the monetization of assets were $222 million ($0.09/share) for the quarter and $588 million ($0.25/share) for the last twelve months.

  • Year-to-date, we have advanced $53 billion of asset sales across the business, including $20 billion during the quarter, realizing significant value for our clients at attractive returns.
  • Monetization activity during the quarter included $4 billion of real estate assets, approximately $5 billion of infrastructure assets, approximately $4 billion of energy assets, $3 billion of private equity investments, and $4 billion of credit investments.
  • As we continue to return capital to our clients through asset monetizations, a number of our funds are moving closer to carried interest realization. Total gross accumulated unrealized carried interest grew by approximately $525 million to $13.0 billion at quarter end, net of realizing $222 million of net carried interest into income during the quarter.

We ended the quarter with $207 billion of capital available to deploy into new investments.

  • Our $207 billion of deployable capital includes $5.4 billion of liquidity at the Corporation, $86.9 billion of cash, financial assets, and undrawn credit lines across our affiliates, and $114.3 billion of uncalled private fund commitments.
  • Our balance sheet is conservatively capitalized, with corporate debt at the Corporation carrying a weighted-average term of 14 years and no remaining maturities in 2026.
  • We have extremely strong access to capital markets, completing $37 billion of financings across the franchise during the quarter. At the Corporation, we issued $600 million of five-year senior unsecured notes at a spread of 82 basis points over the benchmark, one of our lowest spreads ever. The offering was approximately four times oversubscribed, underscoring strong investor demand and the strength of our credit profile.
  • Since we last reported, we repurchased over $300 million of BN Class A shares in the open market, bringing total year-to-date share repurchases to $889 million.

Non-IFRS and Performance Measures

This news release and accompanying financial information are based on IFRS Accounting Standards, as issued by the IASB, unless otherwise noted.

We make reference to Distributable Earnings (“DE”). We define DE as the sum of distributable earnings before realizations from our asset management business and our wealth solutions business, distributions received from our ownership of investments, realized carried interest and realized disposition gains, net of earnings from our Corporate Activities, preferred share dividends and equity-based compensation costs. We also make reference to DE before realizations, which refers to DE before realized carried interest and realized disposition gains. Distributable earnings before realizations from our Asset Management business is comprised of fee-related earnings, realized carried interest, net and investments and other income (expenses), net of cash taxes and equity-based compensation costs from BAM, as well as return on capital from cash distributions received on direct investments. Distributable earnings from our Wealth Solutions business is calculated as net income from our Wealth Solutions business, excluding the impact of depreciation and amortization, deferred income taxes, net income from our equity accounted investments, mark-to-market on investments and derivatives, breakage and transaction costs, and is inclusive of our proportionate share of DE from investments in associates. We believe these measures provide insight into earnings received by the company that are available for distribution to common shareholders or to be reinvested into the business.

Realized carried interest and realized disposition gains are further described below:

  • Realized Carried Interest represents our contractual share of profits generated within a private fund after achieving our clients’ minimum return requirements. Realized carried interest is determined on third-party capital that is no longer subject to future investment performance.
  • Realized Disposition Gains are included in DE because we consider the purchase and sale of assets to be a normal part of the company’s business. Realized disposition gains include gains and losses recorded in net income and equity in the current period, and are adjusted to include fair value changes and revaluation surplus balances recorded in prior periods which were not included in prior period DE, and exclude amounts attributable to direct investments and non-controlling interests unless otherwise noted.

We use DE to assess our operating results and the value of Brookfield Corporation’s business and believe that many shareholders and analysts also find this measure of value to them.

We disclose a number of financial measures in this news release that are calculated and presented using methodologies other than in accordance with IFRS. These financial measures, which include DE, should not be considered as the sole measure of our performance and should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, similar financial measures calculated in accordance with IFRS. We caution readers that these non-IFRS financial measures or other financial metrics are not standardized under IFRS and may differ from the financial measures or other financial metrics disclosed by other businesses and, as a result, may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other issuers and entities.
